## Translator's flags

- **Section = printed 299(mid)–303(top).** A **confession of praise**: the standalone word **PROPTER** ("For / Because of") governs the whole chain of italic accusative headings — *For [His] Excellency of Majesty… His Highness… Eternity… Omnipresence… Omniscience… Omnipotence… Depth of Wisdom… Unshaken truth…* — each attribute proved by a scripture-tag. Render each heading "For [His] …" so the governing *Propter* carries through, and keep the attributes as a rising doxological catalogue (the divine perfections, then the mercies).
- **Layer note (§16/§17):** almost all Latin; two Greek elements, both **main text**, not droppable — the **heading Χρηστός** (302, "Kind/Good/Gracious," Luke 6:35 τοῦ Χρηστοῦ; the divine *chrēstotēs*, with the well-known Χριστός/Χρηστός pun) → kept as the Greek heading with an English gloss; and the **closing Greek acclamation** (303) = Exod 15:11 LXX, the Song of Moses at the Sea, *Τίς ὅμοιός Σοι ἐν Θεοῖς, Κύριε…* ("Who is like unto Thee, O Lord, among the gods? … glorified in holiness, marvellous in glory [praises], doing wonders") — translate in full as the section's climactic praise.
- **The attribute-headings** and their sense: *Celsitudo* (highness — Eccl 5:8 "he that is higher than the highest"), *Æternitas* (Gen 21:33 "the everlasting God"), *Omnipræsentia* (Jer 23:24 "do not I fill heaven and earth?"), *Omniscientia* (Ps 139:7–8 "whither shall I go from thy Spirit?"), *Omnipotentia* (Luke 1:37 "with God nothing shall be impossible"), *Altitudo Sapientiæ* (Rom 11:33 "O the depth of the riches"), *Inconcussa veritas* (unshaken truth), *Exquisita justitia* (perfect/searching righteousness), *Fons, Oceanus, Abyssus misericordiæ* (Fountain, Ocean, Deep of mercy — Ps 42:7), then the mercy-attributes: *Mitis* (meek), *Tolerans/Longanimis* (forbearing/longsuffering), *Misericors* (merciful), *Illibenter puniens* ("punishing unwillingly" — Hos 6:4 "what shall I do unto thee?"), *Compatiens* (Joel 2:13), *Cito desinens* ("soon ceasing [to be angry]"), *Pronus ad veniam* ("ready to pardon"), *Reconciliatio*, *Propitiatio* (the prodigal's robe/ring/fatted calf), *Χρηστός*, *Munificus* (bountiful).
- **The `Munificus` two-column list (303)** is antithetical benefaction — each left act paired with a right: *enlightening the blind ‖ loosing the prisoners, clothing the naked ‖ raising the fallen, upholding the slipping ‖ healing the sick, gathering the scattered ‖ feeding the living, sustaining the hungry ‖ quickening the dead, casting down the proud ‖ lifting the lowly, redeeming the captive ‖ helping in time of trouble* — a compressed Ps 145/146/147 catena of God's works. Keep the two columns parallel.
- Psalm cribs (§9): Ps 117:2 ("the truth of the Lord endureth for ever"), Ps 42:7 ("One deep calleth another"), Ps 78:38 ("so merciful was he, that he… destroyed them not"), Ps 103:9–13 (the whole mercy cluster — "he will not alway be chiding… like as a father pitieth"), and the 303 works-catena from Ps 145/146/147 (openeth the eyes of the blind, looseth men out of prison, raiseth up them that are fallen, healeth the sick, feedeth, etc.). Kept BCP wording.
- `Illibenter puniens` (301) — "punishing unwillingly": God's reluctance to punish (Hos 6:4, "O Ephraim, what shall I do unto thee?" — the divine pathos). A key Andrewes note; render "Unwilling to punish."
- `duplicia pro omnibus peccatis` (302) = Isa 40:2 ("received of the Lord's hand double for all her sins") — the superabundance of grace over sin; kept the AV "double."
