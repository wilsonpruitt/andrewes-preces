## Translator's flags

- **Section = printed 303(mid)–305(top).** The title is **CONFESSIO in …** — "the confession/acknowledgement [of God] IN His Angels and Saints" (God glorified in the hosts of heaven and the Church triumphant). The standalone "in" governs the whole chain of ablatives; render "In [His] Angels… In [His] Archangels…" so the confession reads as praise of God-in-His-creatures.
- **The nine angelic orders** (Dionysian hierarchy), each with its proper act — kept order and function: *Angeli* (bearing charge of men, Heb 1:14), *Archangeli* (announcing greater things by illumination; "the voice of the archangel," 1 Thess 4:16), *Virtutes* (working wonders), *Potestates* (restraining demons by command), *Principatus* (perfecting by governance), *Dominationes* (doing good by largess), *Throni* (judging by session) — with Col 1:16 ("whether thrones, or dominions, or principalities, or powers") as proof — then *Cherubim* (shining with knowledge; Gen 3:24, set before Paradise) and *Seraphim* (burning with love; Isa 6:2, "each one had six wings"). The paired act-nouns (*cognitione / dilectione / jussione / gubernatione / largitione / sessione*) are the ablative-of-means for each order — keep the "by …" so the taxonomy of angelic ministries is legible.
- `Astra matutina / Rectores orbis / Amatores hominum / Summi ministri divinæ voluntatis` — Andrewes' epithets for the angels: "morning stars" (Job 38:7), "rulers/governors of the world," "lovers of men," "chief ministers of the divine will." Then the prayer proper: *Perseverantiam Angelorum… de virtute in virtutem scandendo, eorum choris sociari* — "[grant me] the perseverance of the Angels, mounting from strength to strength (Ps 84:7), to be joined to their choirs."
- **The ranks of the blessed (304)**, each with its distinguishing mark (a compressed martyrology): Patriarchs → *Faith*, Prophets → *Hope*, Apostles (& Evangelists) → *Labours*, Martyrs → *Blood*, Confessors → *Zeal*, Doctors → *Studies/learning*, Ascetics & Therapeutæ → *Tears* (braced together), Virgins → { flowers of purity / heavenly gems / consorts of the spotless Lamb }, Innocents (the Holy Innocents / infants) → { beauty / flowers of the Church / mirrors of virtue / tabernacles of the Holy Ghost }. Keep the two columns aligned and the braces.
- *Therapeutæ* = the ancient ascetic "healers/contemplatives" (Philo's term, paired with the *Ascetæ*) — kept the word (not "therapists"); glossed.
- **Closing braces (304–305):** *Quorum fides fortis, et vita probata* ("whose faith [was] strong, and life approved"), then *Quorum { in corde charitas / in ore veritas / in vita pietas }* ("in whose heart [was] charity, in mouth truth, in life godliness") — the triple summary of sanctity; keep the parallel.
