## Translator's flags

- **Parity 6/6** against the six page-markers (334–339). *Confessio Peccati* — the great Confession of Sin (fresh all-caps heading; §21 ended 333), running as one continuous meditation through the recurring **Behold, O Lord** (*Ecce, Domine*) refrains into the **Meditation of the Day of Judgement**, and closing on the **Daniel 9:19** cadence. The Latin parallel to Part I's Greek Meditatio de judicio. Boundary confirmed: §23 (the Incarnation/economy meditation, *In magnum pietatis sacramentum*) opens mid-339.
- **The confession (334–335).** Penitential-psalm cento, AV/BCP cadence: Ps 69:5 ("thou knowest my foolishness"), Ps 51:3 ("my sin is ever before me"), Job 31:33 ("if I covered my transgressions as Adam"), the *Peccavi / Erravi sicut ovis* refrain (Ps 106:6; Ps 119:176 "I have gone astray like a lost sheep"), the relapse-images (Jer 31:18 untamed heifer; Prov 26:11 & 2 Pet 2:22 dog-to-vomit / washed-sow-to-mire), and Achan's confession (Josh 7:19–20, "give… glory to the LORD… and tell me now what thou hast done"). Ps-lines Coverdale/BCP (§9).
- **The Greek couplet (334).** Matt 12:20 kept as main text and rendered in AV word-order ("smoking flax… bruised reed"), the clauses inverted to match Andrewes' *quench thou not / break thou not*. Its full correct spelling here (*κάλαμον συντετριμμένον μὴ κατεάξῃς*) is noted in the transcript as confirming §20's abbreviated form was a scan defect.
- **The brace catalogues.** Rendered inline to mirror the Latin: *That which I beg of thee {a contrite heart [Ps 51:17] / groanings that cannot be uttered [Rom 8:26] / tears of blood [Luke 22:44]}*; *Woe is me {for the dryness / for the hardness of heart / for the dryness of mine eyes}* (Is 24:16; Rom 2:5; Jer 9:1); the fourfold *{break / rend / prick / smite} my heart*; *{foolish / useless / harmful / dangerous} desire of my flesh*; *{absurdity / baseness / vileness} of my desire* + *[worthy] of {confusion / reproach}*; the judgement two-column table (An incorruptible Judge | A dreadful tribunal …); *None to {stand by / plead for / deliver} me*.
- **The double-sided brace (337).** *{I have deserved death from thee, / Yet still I appeal unto thee,} O Lord, {from thee the just. / to thee the merciful,}* — the chiastic appeal from the throne of justice to the throne of grace (Heb 4:16). Rendered to keep both the left pair (deserved/appeal) and the right pair (just/merciful) legible across the hinge "O Lord."
- **The Day of Judgement (336–338).** The *dies iræ* catalogue — thrones set, books opened (Dan 7:10 / Rev 20:12), the terrors in two columns (the unquenchable fire, the restless worm of Mark 9:44, measureless chaos), the self-sentence, the Mark 4:38 cry ("carest thou not that we perish?"), 1 Tim 2:4 / 2 Pet 3:9 (God willing all saved, none to perish), and the prostration (Luke 18:13 the publican; Ps 130:1 *De profundis*; Ps 143:6 "my soul… as a thirsty land"). AV cadence for the NT/prophetic lines; Coverdale/BCP for the psalms.
- **The Dan 9:19 close (339).** The *Propter*-cascade of mercy (the multitude / riches / abundance / **super**abundance — Ps 51:1, Eph 2:4, 1 Pet 1:3, Rom 5:20) into the publican + "chief of sinners" (Luke 18:13; 1 Tim 1:15), Jas 2:13 ("mercy triumpheth over judgement"), Rom 5:20 ("where sin abounded, grace did much more abound"), and **Daniel 9:19** rendered verbatim in its AV/liturgical form ("O Lord, hear; O Lord, forgive; O Lord, hearken and do; defer not, for thine own sake, O my God"). The tightening indent-cascade preserved — the prayer's most urgent point.
- **Line-keying.** Printed hanging-indent and cascade breaks mirrored between layers; the wrapped verses (Exod-length prose, the 334/338 ref-wraps) keep the same line-count. Brightman/Newman consulted after drafting (§12), not imported.
