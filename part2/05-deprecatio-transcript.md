## Translator's flags

- **Section = printed 280–285.** A *deprecatio* (litany of aversion) headed *Ne perdas* ("Destroy not," Deut. 9:26 / Ps. 75 title Vulg. *ne disperdas*). Two movements: **(a)** the "deliver-us-from" litany with refrains *Libera nos / Propitius esto / Et serva nos* (280–284, the terrors of judgement and the pains of hell), then **(b)** a "grant-us" benediction litany *De frugibus terræ…* (285, harvest, nation, the afflicted). Keep the antiphon-refrain shape.
- **Column tables (rendered ` | `):** 281 (`Paleas… | Dolere…`), 282 (`Inglorium | Ligari, ejici` etc.), 283 (the antithetical `Repelle… | Da…` pairs), 284 (three-column heavenly-goods list). These pair a **privation** against its **remedy or its aggravation** — the English must keep the two columns legible as parallel.
- **Nested / center braces:**
  - 281 `A { mollitie, / duritie } frontis` — "from a *soft* (Prov 7:13, the harlot's brazen face) / *hard* (Isa 48:4, "thy brow brass") **forehead**"; the shared word *frontis* governs both. A deliberate paradox (softness AND hardness of face = shamelessness).
  - 282 `Eripe { … / … }`, `A { lingua dolosa / labiis perversis / laqueis }`, `Ab omnibus inimicis { visibilibus… / corporeis… }`.
  - 283 `Da { Virtutem abstinentiæ / Amorem castitatis / Spiritus paupertatem }` (right column, brace) vs the left-column imperatives; and the trailing `Da { robur fidei / securitatem spei / tutelam salutis }`.
  - 284 `in { tranquillitate / locis { virentibus / rorantibus } / Paradiso / Refrigeratione / Sinu Abrahæ / Tabernaculis Sanctorum }` — a brace nesting a sub-brace (the "green, dewy places" inside the list of blessed abodes). **In the three-column list a printed brace joins the middle column** *Sedere / ad dextram / Dei* **to the right column** *Quies / Honor / Æternitas* — noted here since ` | ` can't show the join; the middle is "to sit at the right hand of God," the right column its fruits.
- **The armour catalogue (284):** *Zonam, Loricam, Calceos, Galeam, Scutum, Gladium, super omnia orationem* = the whole armour of God, Eph 6:14–18 (girdle, breastplate, shoes/greaves, helmet, shield, sword — and "above all, prayer"). Use the AV armour vocabulary.
- **Proper names (282):** *Ashur* (Hos 11:5, Assyria), *Jeroboam* / *Roboam* (Rehoboam; 1 Kings 12 — Vulg. *3 Reg.*, but the 1853 prints `1 Reg.`; kept as printed), *Valle Achor* (Josh 7:24, the valley of Achor / "trouble"), *spiritu malo Sichemitarum* (Judg 9:23, the evil spirit between Abimelech and Shechem). Keep the names; they are a typology of national/spiritual ruin to be delivered from.
- Psalm cribs (§9): Ps 113:5 ("who dwelleth on high, and yet humbleth himself"), Ps 120:2 ("deliver my soul… from a deceitful tongue"), Ps 91:3 ("from the snare of the hunter"), Ps 17:15 ("satisfied… with glory"), and on 285 the harvest cluster Ps 132:15 ("bless her victuals… satisfy her poor with bread"), Ps 147:13–14. Andrewes' *capturam* (132:15, "her provision/catch") kept as "our store/provision."
- `dedolentia postquam peccavimus` (281) = the *ceasing to grieve* after sin (past feeling, Eph 4:19; paired with *cauteriata conscientia*, 1 Tim 4:2, the "seared conscience") — render "past-feeling numbness after we have sinned."
- `Qui dixisti Angelo Tuo percutienti, Sufficit, nunc contine manum Tuam` (283) = 2 Sam 24:16 ("It is enough: stay now thine hand") — the destroying angel of David's plague; kept AV "It is enough."
