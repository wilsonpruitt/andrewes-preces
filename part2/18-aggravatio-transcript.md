# Part II — Preces Quotidianæ — §18 *Aggravatio* + *Peccati Species* — raw transcript

Source: 1853 Parker ed., printed pp. 324(mid)–326(top) (PDF 347–349), images `raw/end/p-NNN.jpg`. Offset **+23**. **LATIN** (no Greek). Two parts of the sin-analysis: **AGGRAVATIO** ("the aggravation" — the nine heads by which sin's gravity is weighed: its measure, quality, iteration [*Quoties?*], continuation [*Quamdiu?*], person [*Quis?* — age/condition/status/knowledge], manner, motive-cause, time, place; then the four aggravating dispositions: folly, ingratitude, hardness, contempt) and **Peccati Species** ("the kinds of sin" — a 16-fold brace-catalogue: cords of sin, necessities/superfluities, omission/commission, of heart/mouth/deed, against God/neighbour/self, ignorant/knowing, unwilling/willing, old/new, by age, once/oft, hidden/manifest, of anger/of lust, before/after calling, sleeping/waking, own/others', remembered/forgotten) closing on the summary plea *peccavi Tibi, Miserere mei, Deus, et dimitte mihi*. ALL brace/two-column layout — the densest brace-page yet. Follows §17 (Peccatorum Confessio, ends 324 after "Et tacuisti"); **Pœnitet (§19) begins mid-326** after "et dimitte mihi." ## Translator's flags

- **Section = printed 324(mid)–326(top).** Two parts of the anatomy of sin: **AGGRAVATIO** (how sin's weight is reckoned) and **Peccati Species** (the kinds of sin). The whole is a scholastic *examen* rendered as brace-diagram — this is the densest brace-page in Part II; the layout IS the content, so keep every brace.
- **AGGRAVATIO — the nine heads (324):** the classic scholastic list of circumstances that aggravate sin — (1) its **measure** (as *harm* [*Noxa*] and *scandal* [*Scandalum*]); (2) **quality**; (3) **iteration** — *how often?* (*Quoties?*); (4) **continuation** — *how long?* (*Quamdiu?*); (5) **person** — *who?* (*Quis?*, unfolding to *age, condition, status, knowledge*); (6) **manner**; (7) **motive-cause**; (8) **time**; (9) **place**. Then the four aggravating **dispositions**: *folly, ingratitude, hardness, contempt* (*Stultitia, Ingratitudo, Duritia, Contemptus*). The right-column interrogatives (*Quoties? Quamdiu? Quis?*) are the diagnostic questions — keep them apposed.
- **Peccati Species — the sixteen kinds (324–326):** a brace-catalogue of the antitheses of sin:
  1. **cords / ropes** — *Funiculi / Rudentes* (Isa 5:18, "cords of vanity… as it were with a cart rope" — sin as the cable that drags);
  2. **necessities / superfluities** (Ps 25:17; Jas 1:21, "superfluity of naughtiness");
  3. **omission / commission** — *not doing what one ought / doing what one ought not* (Rom 7:15, "the good that I would I do not…");
  4. **of the heart** (within, in thought) / **of mouth and deed** (without, in word and act) — the *Cordis/intus/Cogitatione* ‖ *Oris/Operis/extra/Verbo/Facto* double-brace (keep the nesting: inward-thought vs outward-word/work);
  5. **against God / neighbour / one's own body** (1 Cor 6:18);
  6. **ignorant / knowing**;
  7. **unwilling / willing**;
  8. **old / recent**;
  9. **by age** — of *childhood / youth / ripe / advanced* age (*Ex ætate { Pueritiæ / Juventutis / Maturæ / Provectæ } ætatis*);
  10. **once committed / oft repeated**;
  11. **hidden / manifest**;
  12. **of anger / of concupiscence** (of *the flesh / the world*, 1 John 2:16 — inner nested brace);
  13. **by one not yet called / by one already called**;
  14. **sleeping by night / waking by day**;
  15. **one's own / another's** (sins others led me to, or I led them to);
  16. **remembered / forgotten**.
- **The summary plea (326):** *Quæcunque ab infantia usque nunc, usque momentum hoc* — "whatsoever, from infancy until now, unto this very moment" — *knowing or ignorant, within or without, sleeping or waking, in words, deeds, thoughts, by the fiery darts of the enemy* (Eph 6:16), *by the unclean desires of the flesh, I have sinned against Thee* (Ps 51:4, *Tibi soli peccavi*). Closes on *Miserere mei, Deus, et dimitte mihi* — "Have mercy on me, O God, and forgive me." The whole *examen* collapses into the Miserere. Keep the cascading indent (the widening confession) and the plea.
- **No Greek** in this section (the -κλησις/hyper- Greek belongs to §§13–14; Greek returns in §19 *Pœnitet*). Scripture cribs Isaiah/Proverbs/Epistles (AV); Ps 25:17 and 51:4 Coverdale/BCP (§9).
