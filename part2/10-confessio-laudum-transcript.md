## Translator's flags

- **Section = printed 305(mid)–309(top).** *Confessio Laudum* — "the confession of praises," a sustained **Pro { … }** ("For …") thanksgiving that walks the **entire economy of grace**. The braces group each cluster; render "For …" once per brace and let the items hang under it. It is the doxological counterpart to §4 (Gratiarum Actio) but organized by mystery, not by scripture-chain.
- **The clusters, in order:** (1) **the six days + sabbath** (Gen 1 — light, waters/firmament, earth/plants, luminaries, fish/fowl, beasts, holy sabbath); (2) **the making of man** (formed of set counsel and by God's own hands, the divine inbreathing, the image, dominion, angelic guard, Paradise, "sinning yet not forsaken"); (3) **the pedagogy of the nations** (the promised Seed, what-may-be-known-of-God, the law written in hearts, the prophets' oracles, the psalms, the proverbs, the histories); (4) **providence** (birth, nurture, preservation, governance, education, civil order, religion); (5) **the redemption** (Gal 4:7, the great mystery of godliness 1 Tim 3:16, the self-emptying, the humbling, the taking of Abraham's seed, the hypostatic union, the offering of life, the sacrifice of death); (6) **"for all He did and suffered, from the manger to the cross"**; (7) **the mysteries of Christ's life** (Incarnation → poor Nativity → manger → Circumcision-under-the-Law → firstfruits of blood → the lovely Name of JESUS → manifestation to the Gentile sinners [the Magi] → Presentation → Flight into Egypt → the boy in the Temple: eager to hear, eager to ask, humbly obedient to His parents); (8) **the public life** (Baptism, the Trinity's appearing there, the Fast, the Temptation, His homelessness, hunger & thirst, cold & heat, constant weariness "as He went about doing good," His watchings and all-night prayers); (9) **the reproaches** — that He willed to be *called* Samaritan / glutton / demoniac / deceiver, and *postponed to Barabbas*; (10) **His teaching offices** (sermons/homilies, colloquies/diatribes, intercessions/prayers, examples, signs, mysteries, the keys); (11) **His parables of mercy** (the two debtors, the man half-dead, the Publican & Pharisee, the servant in debt, the straying sheep, the lost coin, the prodigal, the eleventh-hour hired); (12) **His mercy-sayings** (*Pro Sententiis* — "God sent not His Son to judge but to save," "I came not to call the righteous," "Come unto Me all ye that labour," "him that cometh I will in no wise cast out," "Father, forgive them," "To day… in Paradise"); (13) **His examples of mercy** (*Pro Exemplis* — the Canaanite/Syrophoenician woman, the Samaritan woman, the woman with the issue, the adulteress, Mary Magdalene, Zacchaeus, the thief, Peter, Thomas, Paul, Nicodemus).
- **Inline Greek (309):** `Οὗτος δέχεται ἁμαρτωλούς` (Luke 15:2, "This man receiveth sinners") with its own Latin gloss *Hic peccatores recipit* — the Pharisees' sneer turned into the gospel's glory, the hinge of the whole *Pro Exemplis* list. Keep the Greek + English; the Latin gloss is Andrewes'/the edition's own.
- **`Pro omnibus { bonis quæ fecit / malis quæ passus est } { a præsepi usque / ad crucem }`** (306): the great summary — "For all the good He did and the evil He suffered, from the manger even to the cross." A double brace (deeds/sufferings × from-crib/to-cross); keep both.
- **`{ Samaritanus / Helluo / Dæmoniacus / Planus } vocari`** (307): the four titles of reproach Christ accepted — Samaritan (John 8:48), *Helluo* = "glutton/wine-bibber" (Matt 11:19), demoniac (John 7:20), *Planus* = "impostor/deceiver" (Matt 27:63, πλάνος). Keep the brace; render *Helluo* "a gluttonous man," *Planus* "a deceiver."
- **The `Pro Concionibus { … }` two-column (307):** Christ's modes of ministry paired — sermons/homilies, colloquies/diatribes (discourses), intercessions/prayers — then examples, signs, mysteries (sacraments), keys. Kept the pairing.
- `Amabili nomine JESU` (306) = "the lovely/adorable Name of JESUS" — the Holy Name devotion (kept caps JESU per §10).
- `Manifestatione peccatoribus de Gentibus` (306, Matt 2:11) = the Epiphany to the Magi ("the Gentile sinners") — Andrewes reads the Wise Men as the firstfruits of the Gentiles; render "His manifestation to the sinners of the Gentiles."
- Scripture cribs mostly Gospel/Epistle (AV); the two-column examples are proper names — keep them (a gallery of the forgiven). `Muliere hæmorrhoïssa` = "the woman with the issue of blood."
