## Translator's flags

- **Parity 3/3.** A confession of God glorified in His whole company — the nine angelic orders and the ranks of the saints. Kept the governing "in [His] …" so it reads as praise of God-in-His-hosts.
- **The nine orders** (Dionysian; each with its ministry, ablative-of-means → "by/with …"): Angels bear men's charge (Heb 1:14), Archangels announce by illumination (1 Thess 4:16 "the voice of the archangel"), Virtues work wonders, Powers restrain demons by command, Principalities perfect by governance, Dominions do good by largess, Thrones judge by session — Col 1:16 as warrant — then Cherubim shine with knowledge (Gen 3:24) and Seraphim burn with love (Isa 6:2). Kept the knowledge/love pairing of the two highest orders (the Cherub = illumined mind, the Seraph = kindled will), a commonplace Andrewes leans on.
- `Astra matutina` = "morning stars" (Job 38:7, the angels shouting for joy at creation); `Rectores orbis` / `Amatores hominum` / `Summi ministri divinæ voluntatis` — angelic epithets kept literally. The petition *Perseverantiam Angelorum… de virtute in virtutem scandendo, eorum choris sociari* draws Ps 84:7 ("from strength to strength") — grant to persevere as the angels do (who did not fall) and at last to join their choirs.
- **The ranks of the blessed (304)** — a compressed martyrology, each with its badge: Patriarchs/Faith, Prophets/Hope, Apostles (& Evangelists)/Labours, Martyrs/Blood, Confessors/Zeal, Doctors/Studies (learning), Ascetics & *Therapeutæ*/Tears, Virgins/{purity's flowers, heavenly gems, consorts of the Lamb}, Innocents (the Holy Innocents)/{beauty, flowers of the Church, mirrors of virtue, tabernacles of the Spirit}. Kept the columns aligned; the Evangelists share the Apostles' "Labours" (blank right column).
- *Therapeutæ* left untranslated (the ancient contemplative "healers," Philo's Egyptian ascetics, paired with the *Ascetæ*) — not "therapists"; glossed in transcript.
- **The closing triads:** *Quorum fides fortis, et vita probata* → "Whose faith was strong, and life approved"; *Quorum { in corde charitas / in ore veritas / in vita pietas }* → "In whose heart was charity, mouth truth, life godliness" — the threefold seal of holiness (heart/mouth/life). Kept the brace and parallel.
