## Translator's flags

- **Parity 2/2** against the two page-markers (315 mid, 316). *Transfiguratio* rendered "The Transfiguring" — Christ's **glorification in His exaltation** (the counterpart to §11's Passion), not the Mount Tabor event. The eight items are the triumph proper: the spoiling of the powers (Col 2:15), the resurrection, the appearances, the ascension, the session, the gift-giving, the intercession, the return to judgment.
- **Item 1** *Triumphus et traductio principatuum … in seipso* renders Col 2:15 closely — *traductio* = the leading of captives in a triumphal procession ("made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it/Himself"). Kept "leading in procession."
- **The ten appearances** given in Andrewes' order (harmonizing 1 Cor. 15:5—8 with the Gospels): Magdalene, the women, Peter, Emmaus, the Ten (Thomas absent), the Eleven (Thomas present), Tiberias, James, the Five hundred, Bethany. *Decem sine Thoma* / *Undecim* rendered "the Ten without Thomas" / "the Eleven" to keep the two upper-room appearances distinct. *In Bethania* (Luke 24:50) = the Ascension scene, closing the appearances.
- **No Greek** in this section; a clean Latin stretch.
