## Translator's flags

- **Parity 5/5.** The governing **PROPTER** ("For / Because of") carries into every italic heading — "For the Excellency of His Majesty… For His Highness… For His Eternity…" — a rising doxology of the divine perfections (majesty, highness, eternity, omnipresence, omniscience, omnipotence, wisdom, truth, righteousness) then the mercies (meekness, forbearance, mercy, reluctance to punish, compassion, readiness to pardon, reconciliation, propitiation, kindness, bounty). Kept the "For [His] …" so the whole reads as one act of praise.
- **The two Greek elements (main text, kept):** the heading **Χρηστός** (302) — the divine *chrēstotēs* (Luke 6:35, "kind to the unthankful and evil"); glossed "Χρηστός — Kind," and note the famous **Χριστός/Χρηστός** homophone (Christ / the Kind One) Andrewes surely intends. The closing **Exod 15:11** acclamation (303) — the Song of Moses at the Red Sea, translated in full ("Who is like unto Thee, O Lord, among the gods? glorified in holiness, marvellous in praises, doing wonders") as the section's climax. `ἐν δόξαις` rendered "in praises" (LXX sense; AV Exod 15:11 "fearful in praises").
- **`Illibenter puniens`** (301) — "unwilling to punish": God's reluctance, voiced by Hos 6:4's "O Ephraim, what shall I do unto thee?" — the divine pathos over judgement. A signature Andrewes note.
- **`Munificus` two-column list (303):** a compressed Ps 145/146/147 catena of God's works, each left act answered by a right — enlighten/loose, clothe/raise, uphold/heal, gather/feed, sustain/quicken, cast-down/lift-up, redeem/help. Kept the antithetical pairing and the BCP verbs ("openeth the eyes of the blind… looseth the prisoners… raiseth up them that are fallen"). *Pro opera horaria mercedem diurnam* (Matt 20:9) = the eleventh-hour labourers paid a full day — the bounty that overpays.
- Psalm cribs (§9): Ps 139:7–8 ("whither shall I go from thy Spirit… if I climb up into heaven… if I go down to hell"), 117:2, 111:3, 42:7 ("One deep calleth another"), 78:38 ("so merciful was he… destroyed them not"), 103:9–13 (the mercy cluster), and the 303 works-catena. Kept BCP verbatim.
- `Suscepit… duplicia pro omnibus peccatis` (302) = Isa 40:2 ("received of the Lord's hand double for all her sins") — grace superabounding over sin; kept "double."
- `Excellentiam Majestatis` count (299) puts **Christ's own words** (John 17:5, "glorify me… with the glory I had") as the first ground of praise — the eternal Son's majesty; kept the high-priestly-prayer wording, and Melchizedek (Gen 14:18) as the type of His eternal priesthood.
