## Translator's flags

- **Section = printed 275(title)–279.** A single sustained *thanksgiving* built as a chain of `Qui …` relative clauses (Thou who …), each a scripture-tag. It rehearses: creation ex nihilo (Rom 4:17, Col 1:16, Heb 1:3), providence (Acts 14:17, Ps 119:91), the making and dignifying of man (Gen 2:7, 1:26, Ps 8:6, Gen 2:15), the Fall answered by the protevangelium (Acts 11:18, 2 Pet 1:4 / Gen 3:15), the pedagogy of the nations (law/sacrifice/prophets/psalms/proverbs/history), the Incarnation and redemption (Gal 4:4, Phil 2:7, Gal 3:13), the spread of the Gospel (2 Cor 2:14, Heb 2:4), then Church, King, nation, and the self's own baptism and preservation.
- **Psalm cribs (§9):** Ps 145:10 ("All thy works praise thee… and thy saints give thanks unto thee"), Ps 92:1–2 ("It is a good thing to give thanks… to tell of thy loving-kindness early in the morning, and of thy truth in the night-season"), Ps 145:1–2 ("I will magnify thee, O God my King… every day will I give thanks unto thee"), Ps 147:14 ("filleth thee with the flour of wheat"), Ps 144:14 ("no decay, no leading into captivity, and no complaining in our streets"), Ps 16:11 ("thou shalt shew me the path of life"), Ps 66:8 ("who holdeth our soul in life, and suffereth not our feet to slip"). Keep BCP wording where it agrees with the Vulgate.
- **Two right-joining braces (277):** `{ Morte sua redimens / Resurrectione vivificans } genus nostrum` (by His death redeeming, by His resurrection quickening, our race — Rom 4:25); `{ nullius auctoritatis / nullius persuasionis } interventu` (by the intervention of no [worldly] authority, no [human] persuasion — i.e. the world's conversion owed nothing to force or rhetoric). Keep both items joined to the shared right-hand word.
- **Lettered footnote apparatus (279):** the four superscripts ᵃᵇᶜᵈ key *confitenti / roganti / mysteriorum / clavium* to 1 John 1:9, Matt 18:32, John 20:23, Matt 16:19 — the door of hope opened to the *confessing* and *asking* soul, out of the power of the *mysteries* (sacraments) and the *keys*. A rare Andrewes cross-hatch; preserve the letters and the collected ref-line.
- `Qui non præscidisti, sicut textor, vitam meam` (279) = Isa 38:12 (Hezekiah's canticle, "I have cut off like a weaver my life") — God has NOT cut the half-woven web; render with the weaver image intact.
- `dissimulasti peccata mea propter pœnitentiam` (278) = Wisdom 11:23 (Vulg. *dissimulas peccata hominum propter pœnitentiam*, "thou winkest at the sins of men, that they should amend"). `Sap.` = Sapientia / Wisdom (deuterocanonical) — keep the abbreviation, note the book.
- `firmamentum veritatis` / `depositum` / `viam pacis` (278): 1 Tim 3:15, 1 Tim 6:20 ("keep that which is committed to thy trust"), Rom 5:1 — kept the AV theological terms ("pillar and ground of the truth," "the deposit / that committed trust").
