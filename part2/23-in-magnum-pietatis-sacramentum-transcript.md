## Transcriber's flags

- **Parity 7/7** page-markers (339 foot, 340, 341, 342, 343, 344, 345 top). Offset **+23** verified by eye on every leaf; no duplicate leaves in this run (the tail duplicates warned of in STRUCTURE.md have not begun yet at 345).
- **Layer: pure Latin.** Not one Greek word in the whole section — the first sustained Latin-only stretch since §21. (The Greek returns later in Part II at the hymns/poems finale per STRUCTURE §PARS SECUNDA.)
- **The 339/340 join is syntactically defective as printed.** 339 ends *Dominum nostrum,* and 340 opens *qua Creator, / qua Redemptor, / Sed efficaciter et manifeste operantem.* — the accusative *operantem* and the *qua … qua … Sed* construction want an antecedent (the Holy Ghost) and almost certainly a *non tantum* ("not only as Creator, not only as Redeemer, **but** working effectually and manifestly"), which the 1853 page does not carry. **Not a scan defect**: the leaves are consecutive (339 = PDF 362, 340 = PDF 363, both eye-verified) and the Google OCR text runs the same sequence, so the omission is in the printing, not in our reading. Check against Brightman/the Harley MS before print; do not silently repair.
- **`[Jer. l. 24.]`** (printed 342) — the reference is lower-case **L** = Jer. **50**:24 (*"thou art taken… because thou hast striven against the LORD"*, matching *obnitar*), not "Jer. 1." The 1853 fount makes the two nearly identical; recorded here so a later pass does not "correct" it wrongly.
- **`in Sancta Communionis simus participes`** (printed 342) — as printed; the concord is broken (expected *in Sanctorum Communione* or *Sanctæ Communionis*). Transcribed verbatim; the following genitive plural *Sanctarum { Personarum / Actionum / Precum / Liturgiæ }* shows the sense intended (partaking of the holy things *and* of the communion of saints).
- **Two ref calls to check on a cleaner scan** (both legible here, but odd as citations): *non temporariam* cited `[Apoc. ii. 13.]` where Luke viii. 13 is the obvious locus, and *liquida scientia* cited `[2 Cor. ii. 6.]` where ii. 14 (*"the savour of his knowledge"*) fits. Transcribed as printed.
- **The Abaddon table (341).** Five dative/accusative antithesis-rows, transcribed as two columns ` | `; the left members are datives governed by *opponit* (against the destroyer, Satan/Adversary, Devil/Slanderer, Accuser, and him that leadeth us captive) and the right members accusatives (Jesus, Mediator, Advocate, Intercessor, Redeemer). Two of the left cells are printed as right-joining braces `{ … }` around a name-pair.
- **The doxology grid (345).** Printed as a true four-column table across three rows (twelve members of Rev. v. 12–13 / vii. 12); kept as three ` | ` rows in printed order, reading **across**, with the *et* left at the end of row two exactly as set.
- **Movement breaks.** The 1853 sets blank lines (no heading, no rule) before *Domine, adauge mihi fidem* (342) and before *O qui arundinem quassatam* (343). Marked as movement breaks, **not** section breaks — the section's only hard boundary is the all-caps CONFESSIO LAUDIS at mid-345. Read one page past confirmed this (§8-lesson observed).
