## Translator's flags

- **Parity 5/5.** A chain of `Qui …` clauses → "Who …" (Thou who …); kept the relative-clause litany form throughout so the thanksgiving reads as one breath.
- **Psalm/canticle cribs (§9), BCP wording where it agrees with the Vulgate:** Ps 145:10 ("Let Thy works praise Thee… let Thy saints give thanks"), Ps 92:1–2 ("It is a good thing to give thanks… to tell of Thy loving-kindness in the morning, and of Thy truth in the night-season"), Ps 145:1–2 ("I will magnify Thee, O God my King… Every day will I give thanks"), Ps 147:13–14 ("made fast the bars of thy gates… blessed thy children within thee… maketh peace in thy borders… flour of wheat" — Andrewes reverses vv. 14/13, order preserved), Ps 144:14 ("no decay, no leading into captivity, and no complaining in our streets"), Ps 105:22 ("teach his senators wisdom"), Ps 21:6 ("glad with the joy of thy countenance"), Ps 66:8 ("holdeth our soul in life… suffereth not our feet to slip"). Kept BCP verbatim.
- **Non-Psalter AV echoes:** Rom 4:17 ("calleth those things which be not as though they were"), Heb 1:3 ("upholding all things by the word of his power"), Acts 14:17 ("left not himself without witness… filling our hearts with food and gladness"), Gen 2:7 ("breathed into his nostrils the breath of life"), Phil 2:7 ("made himself of no reputation… took upon him the form of a servant"), Gal 3:13 ("the curse of the law"), 2 Pet 1:4 ("partakers of the divine nature"), 2 Cor 2:14 ("the savour of his knowledge in every place"), 1 Tim 3:15 ("the pillar and ground of the truth"), Isa 2:4 ("swords into plowshares… spears into pruninghooks"), Titus 3:5 ("the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost").
- `præhabita deliberatione` (276) → "of set counsel" (God's deliberate resolve before forming man, cf. "Let us make man") — kept the deliberative sense.
- **Right-joining braces (277):** `{ By His death redeeming / By His resurrection quickening } our race` (Rom 4:25, "delivered for our offences… raised again for our justification"); `by the intervention of { no worldly authority / no human persuasion }` — the world converted without force or rhetoric. `[worldly]` / `[human]` supplied in brackets to disambiguate *nullius auctoritatis / persuasionis*.
- **Lettered apparatus (279):** kept superscripts ᵃᵇᶜᵈ on *confessing / asking / mysteries / keys*, refs normalized to modern form (1 John 1:9; Matt 18:32; John 20:23; Matt 16:19). The door of hope opened to the confessing-and-asking soul, from the power of sacraments (*mysteria*) and the keys — a compact statement of Andrewes' sacramental penitence.
- `Qui non præscidisti, sicut textor, vitam meam… dum adhuc ordirer` (279) = Isa 38:12 (Hezekiah, "I have cut off like a weaver my life"); rendered with the loom image ("cut off… while yet I was but at the beginning of the web").
- `dissimulasti peccata mea propter pœnitentiam` (278) = Wisd. 11:23 (Vulg. "thou winkest at the sins of men, that they should amend") — kept "winked at… that I might repent." `Sap.` = Wisdom (deuterocanonical).
- `Pro hisce omnibus, etc.` (279): Andrewes' own "&c." — the thanksgiving is open-ended; kept the abbreviation.
