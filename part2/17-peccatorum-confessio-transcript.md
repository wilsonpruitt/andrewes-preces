# Part II — Preces Quotidianæ — §17 *Peccatorum Confessio* — raw transcript

Source: 1853 Parker ed., printed pp. 323(mid)–324(top) (PDF 346–347), images `raw/end/p-NNN.jpg`. Offset **+23**. **LATIN** (no Greek). Title **PECCATORUM CONFESSIO** ("the confession of sins"), opening **Peccavi** ("I have sinned"): the confession proper — first of **original sin** (born of unclean seed and unclean womb, conceived in sins Ps 51:5, a root of bitterness, a shoot of the wild olive), then a **fourteen-fold catalogue** of actual sin in a downward progression (I sinned → dealt unjustly → cast off the law → grieved the Spirit → went after my own thoughts → passed from evil to evil → feared Thee not → returned not → was not even called back → not even chastened → but hardened → provoked Thee) closing on the terrible *Et hæc omnia vidisti, et tacuisti* ("and Thou sawest all this, and heldest Thy peace"). One brace (semine/utero) + a short cascading close. Follows §16 (Allegatio fragilitatis, ends 323 after "Memor esto hujus"); **AGGRAVATIO (§18) begins mid-324** after "Et tacuisti." ## Translator's flags

- **Section = printed 323(mid)–324(top).** *Peccatorum Confessio* — "the Confession of Sins," the confession proper of Part II's penitential cycle, opening with the bare *Peccavi* ("I have sinned"). Two parts: the confession of **original** sin, then the **fourteen-fold** catalogue of **actual** sin.
- **The opening (323):** *Vere, Domine, et ego ex illis sum, nam et vita mea manifestum me facit* — "Truly, Lord, I too am one of them, for my very life makes me manifest" (cf. Matt 26:73, "thy speech bewrayeth thee"). *Tibi confiteor, quia, si volo abscondere, non possum a Te* — "To Thee I confess, for though I would hide, I cannot [hide] from Thee" (Ps 139). Render the plain penitential prose directly.
- **Original sin (323):** *Quis potest facere mundum de immundo semine?* (Job 14:4, "Who can bring a clean thing out of an unclean?"). *Peccator sum { ex immundo semine / ex immundo utero }* — "I am a sinner, of unclean seed, of unclean womb" (keep the brace). *In peccatis concepit me mater* (Ps 51:5, "in sin did my mother conceive me"). Then two graft-images: *Radix amaritudinis* ("a root of bitterness," Heb 12:15) and *Oleastri surculus* ("a shoot of the wild olive," Rom 11:17 — the *oleaster*, the uncultivated stock, the opposite of the good olive into which the believer is grafted). These name the fallen nature as bitter root and wild scion.
- **The fourteen-fold catalogue (323–324):** a downward progression, each with its text — (1) I have sinned, dealt unjustly, done iniquity before Thee (Ps 106:6, the great confession); (2) dealt wickedly in the covenant (1 Kgs 8:47); (3) cast away the law (Isa 5:24); (4) rejected instruction (Prov 15:32); (5) grieved/oppressed the Spirit (Isa 63:10); (6) walked after my own thoughts/imaginations (Jer 18:12, "we will walk after our own devices"); (7) passed from evil to evil (Ps 69:27 / Jer 9:3); (8) feared Thee not (Isa 57:11); (9) returned not (Luke 15:20, the prodigal not yet risen); (10) not even when called back (Jer 7:13, "I called… but ye answered not"); (11) not even when afflicted (Jer 5:3, "thou hast stricken them, but they have not grieved"; Prov 23:35, "they have beaten me… and I felt it not"); (12) but hardened myself (Heb 3:13, "hardened through the deceitfulness of sin"); (13) provoked Thee (1 Kgs 16:33, Ahab's provoking; Ps 10:13, "wherefore doth the wicked… contemn God?").
- **The close (324):** *(14) Et hæc omnia vidisti* (Lam 3:59, "O Lord, thou hast seen my wrong") — *Et tacuisti* (Ps 50:21, "these things hast thou done, and I kept silence"). The dreadful turn: God saw every sin of the fourteen and **held His peace** — His silence not indifference but forbearance (the same Ps 50:21 that continues "thou thoughtest that I was altogether such an one as thyself"). Keep the two lines as the section's hinge into the *Aggravatio*. §17 ends; **Aggravatio (§18)** — the weighing of sin's gravity — follows.
- **No Greek.** Psalm-lines Coverdale/BCP (§9); the catalogue is a prophets-and-Wisdom catena (AV). *1 Reg.* = 1 Kings (Vulgate numbering, = 1/2 Sam + 1/2 Kgs as 1–4 Reg.); here viii.47 = 1 Kgs 8:47 (Solomon's prayer) and xvi.33 = 1 Kgs 16:33 (Ahab).
