## Translator's flags

- **Parity 6/6.** Two movements: the *Libera nos / Propitius esto / Et serva nos* aversion-litany (280–284) and the *De frugibus terræ* benediction-litany (285). Kept the refrain-antiphon shape; the terse "From … / Deliver us" petitions read as a spoken litany.
- **`sed magis / A Tuo non irasci`** (280) — the theological crux: worse than God's *wrath* is God's *ceasing to be angry* (Hosea's "let him alone," Rom 1:24 abandonment — God giving the sinner up as beyond correction). Rendered "but rather / From Thy ceasing to be angry" and flagged; this is the sharp point of the whole deprecation and must not be softened into a mere "from Thy not being angry with us" (which would invert the sense).
- **The column tables** are privation-against-remedy/aggravation. 281 (`Chaff… | To grieve…`) and 282 (`Inglorious | To be bound…`) pile hell's places against its pains; 283 pairs each imperative *remove* (left) with its *give* (right) — a classic Andrewes antithesis (drive off gluttony → give abstinence, quench worldly coveting → give poverty of spirit). Kept the two columns parallel line-for-line.
- **`A { mollitie / duritie } frontis`** (281): the paradox of a *soft* forehead (Prov 7:13 LXX/Vulg, the harlot's shameless face) AND a *hard* forehead (Isa 48:4, "thy brow brass") — both = brazen shamelessness. Kept both adjectives braced to "forehead."
- **Heavenly-goods three-column list (284):** the printed brace joins the middle column *to sit at the right hand of God* (Sedere / ad dextram / Dei) to the right column *Rest / Honour / Eternity* — its fruits. Since ` | ` can't render the join, noted in the transcript; the English keeps the three columns aligned. *Feriari* → "to keep holy-day" (the eternal sabbath-rest, Heb 4:9). *Ter-Sanctus* = the Thrice-Holy (Sanctus/Trisagion).
- **Armour of God (284):** girdle / breastplate / shoes / helmet / shield / sword / "above all, prayer" — Eph 6:14–18, AV vocabulary. Kept "above all" for *super omnia* (which is both "over all [the armour]" and "above all [in importance]").
- **Proper-name typology (282):** Asshur (Assyria, Hos 11:5), Jeroboam & Rehoboam (the schism of the kingdom, 1 Kings 12 — 1853 prints `1 Reg.`, i.e. the Vulgate's *3 Reg.*; kept as printed but noted), the valley of Achor (Josh 7:24, "trouble"), the evil spirit of the Shechemites (Judg 9:23). Andrewes prays deliverance from these as types of division, treachery, and judged sin — kept the names, glossed in the transcript.
- Psalm cribs (§9): Ps 113:5 ("dwelleth on high… humbleth himself to behold"), Ps 120:2 ("deceitful tongue"), Ps 91:3 ("snare of the hunter"), Ps 17:15 ("satisfied… with glory"), and the 285 harvest cluster Ps 132:15 ("bless her victuals… satisfy her poor with bread"), Ps 147:13–14 ("made fast the bars of thy gates… flour of wheat"). *Capturam* (132:15) → "store/provision."
- `dedolentia postquam peccavimus` (281) = ceasing to grieve after sin (Eph 4:19 "past feeling," with the seared conscience of 1 Tim 4:2) — "being past feeling after we have sinned."
- `Bullitionibus Belial` (281) — "the seethings/boilings of Belial" (the bubbling cauldron of hell); kept the vivid *bulliti-* image.
