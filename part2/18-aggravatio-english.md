## Translator's flags

- **Parity 3/3** against the three page-markers (324 mid, 325, 326 top). Two parts of the *examen*: **The Aggravation** (how sin's weight is reckoned) and **The Kinds of Sin**. A scholastic diagram; I keep the brace-form in English because the structure is the meditation.
- **The Aggravation (324):** the nine circumstances that heighten guilt — measure (as *harm* and *scandal*), quality, iteration (*how often?*), continuance (*how long?*), person (*who?* — with age/condition/status/knowledge), manner, moving cause, time, place. Then the four aggravating dispositions: **folly, ingratitude, hardness, contempt**. The right-column questions (*How often? How long? Who?*) are the examining voice — kept apposed.
- **The Kinds of Sin (324–326):** sixteen antithetical pairs. Notable renderings: **1** *Funiculi / Rudentes* = "cords / cart-ropes" (Isa 5:18, "that draw iniquity with cords of vanity, and sin as it were with a cart rope"); **2** *Superfluitates* = "superfluities" (Jas 1:21, "superfluity of naughtiness"); **3** the omission/commission pair glossed with its own definitions (*not doing what one ought / doing what one ought not*, Rom 7:15); **4** the heart/mouth-deed double-brace — inward (*of the heart, within, in thought*) vs outward (*of mouth and deed, without, in word and act*), the nesting kept; **5** against God / neighbour / one's own body (1 Cor 6:18); **12** the anger/concupiscence pair with the nested *of the flesh / of the world* (1 John 2:16). The pairs together map the whole surface of sin — inward and outward, willing and unwilling, hidden and open, at every age.
- **The summary plea (326):** the great collapse — *whatsoever, from infancy until now, unto this very moment; knowing or ignorant, within or without, sleeping or waking, in words, deeds, thoughts, by the fiery darts of the enemy* (Eph 6:16), *by the unclean desires of the flesh, I have sinned against Thee* (Ps 51:4, "against thee only have I sinned"). Then the Miserere: *Have mercy on me, O God, and forgive me.* The entire diagram resolves into the one prayer. The cascading indent (the confession widening as it descends) is kept as the section's closing shape. §18 ends; *Pœnitet* (§19) — the acts of penitence, Greek-rich again — follows.
- **No Greek** here; Isaiah/Proverbs/Epistle cribs (AV), Ps 25:17 & 51:4 Coverdale/BCP.
