## Translator's flags

- **Parity 5/5.** The great **"For …" thanksgiving** over the whole economy of grace — creation, man's making, the pedagogy of the nations, providence, redemption, and the mysteries of Christ (life → ministry → mockery → teaching → parables → sayings → examples). Kept "For" once per brace, the items hanging under it, so the doxology sweeps.
- **`For all { the good He did / the evil He suffered } { from the manger even / to the cross }`** (306): the pivot summary — a double brace (deeds ‖ sufferings × crib → cross). Kept both halves so the compression lands.
- **The mysteries of Christ's life** in AV terms: the "poor Nativity," the manger (Luke 2:7), the Circumcision that "subjected Him to the Law" (Gal 4:4), "the firstfruits of His blood," "the lovely Name of JESUS" (the Holy Name devotion, caps per §10), "the manifestation to the sinners of the Gentiles" (the Magi/Epiphany, Matt 2:11), Presentation, Flight, and the twelve-year-old's threefold temper — eager to hear, eager to ask, humbly obedient (Luke 2:46–51).
- **The reproaches (307):** the four accepted slanders braced — Samaritan (John 8:48), *Helluo* → "a gluttonous man" (Matt 11:19, "a gluttonous man, and a winebibber"), demoniac (John 7:20), *Planus* → "a deceiver" (Matt 27:63, πλάνος) — and "postponed to Barabbas" (John 18:40). Kept the AV "gluttonous man" and "deceiver."
- **`Pro Sententiis`** rendered as the AV **verbatim dominical sayings** — these are the actual Gospel words (John 3:17; 12:47; Matt 9:13; Luke 9:56; 19:10; Matt 20:28; 11:28; John 6:37; Luke 23:34,43; Matt 20:14). Used AV wording exactly ("I will in no wise cast out," "a ransom for many," "Father, forgive them").
- **Inline Greek (309):** `Οὗτος δέχεται ἁμαρτωλούς` (Luke 15:2, the Pharisees' complaint "This man receiveth sinners") — the gospel-in-a-line that heads the whole *Pro Exemplis* gallery of the forgiven; kept the Greek with the AV gloss (the edition supplies the Latin *Hic peccatores recipit*, which I render as the AV verse).
- **`Pro Exemplis`** — a gallery of Christ's mercy: rendered "In [the case of] …" for each ablative (the Canaanite/Syrophoenician woman, the Samaritan woman, the woman with the issue, the adulteress, Mary Magdalene, Zacchaeus, the thief, Peter, Thomas, Paul, Nicodemus), then His forbearance toward His persecutors (gainsayers, would-be stoners, blasphemers, the Barabbas-crowd, "His crucifiers of the Gentiles"). Kept the two movements (the forgiven, then the borne-with).
- `Muliere hæmorrhoïssa` (309) = "the woman with the issue of blood" (ἡ αἱμορροοῦσα); `Adultera deprehensa` = "the woman taken in adultery" (John 8:11 — the *pericope adulterae*). Kept the AV designations.
