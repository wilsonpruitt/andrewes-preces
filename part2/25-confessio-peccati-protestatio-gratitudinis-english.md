## Translator's flags

1. **Title.** *Confessio Peccati* is Englished **the Confession of Sin**, matching §22 which carries the same heading — but the two are unlike: §22 enumerates, this one does not. Here confession is made by self-abasement (Jacob, David) and turns at *Quid retribuam* into thanksgiving, which is why the sub-heading *et Protestatio Gratitudinis* is not an afterthought but the section's method. It stands opposite §24's *Confession of Praise* on the same opening: praise for what God made him, then what he has made of it.

2. **Gen. 32:10 translated from the Latin, not the AV.** *Minor sum omnibus miserationibus Tuis* = "I am **less than** all thy mercies"; the AV reads "I am not worthy of the least of all the mercies." Andrewes' comparative is stronger and is kept. *Explevisti* = "hast fulfilled," not the AV's "hast shewed."

3. **2 Sam. 9:8 is a cento.** The Latin fuses 9:8 ("such a dead dog as I am" — AV kept) with *et quæ domus mea* from 7:18 ("and what is my house"), which the AV of 9:8 does not carry. Both members translated; not an error to be corrected away.

4. **`Quid retribuam` (Ps. 116:12) in Coverdale.** "What reward shall I give unto the Lord: for all the benefits that he hath done unto me?" (BCP Psalter) — Andrewes' Latin has *pro omnibus quæ tribuit mihi*, so "for all that he hath done unto me," Coverdale's cadence without his "benefits." Per §9 the BCP Psalter governs the psalm-lines.

5. **The self-oblation is the Anaphora, and must sound like it.** *Suscipe spirituale hoc sacrificium … in spirituale Tuum Altare … digneris vicem ejus mittere in me gratiam SS. Spiritus Tui* is the prayer of oblation from the Greek liturgies. Rendered in the same Divine-Liturgy English settled for Part I's communion devotions (printed 238–248): "Do thou thyself, O Lord, receive … upon thy spiritual Altar … vouchsafe to send down upon me in return the grace of thy Most Holy Spirit." *Vicem ejus* = "in return **for it**" — the exchange is the point: the sacrifice goes up, the grace comes down. **`SS.` = *Sanctissimi*, Most Holy** — singular, not a plural abbreviation.

6. **`tam voluntarium quam involuntarium`** — the liturgical pair "voluntary and involuntary" (ἑκούσιον καὶ ἀκούσιον) from the Greek absolutions. Kept verbatim; not softened to "witting and unwitting," which loses the formula.

7. **`spiritum, animum et corpus`** — the trichotomy of 1 Thess. 5:23, given in AV words ("spirit, and soul, and body"). But Andrewes' middle term is *animum* (mind, the seat of will), not *anima*: noted rather than pressed into the English, which has no way to carry it without a gloss.

8. **Eph. 3:21 as Andrewes has it.** "Unto him be glory **in the Church and in Christ**" — his Latin has *et*, the AV "in the church **by** Christ Jesus." Translated as printed. *In omnes generationes sæcula sæculorum* rendered "unto all generations, world without end" (the AV's "throughout all ages, world without end").

9. **Ps. 63:5 is Coverdale nearly verbatim** — "My soul shall be satisfied, even as it were with marrow and fatness: when my mouth praiseth thee with joyful lips" (BCP). The Latin's two clauses are coordinate (*et labiis … laudabit os meum*), so "and my mouth shall praise thee" rather than Coverdale's "when."

10. **The brace over petitions 1 and 2 cannot survive into English.** The 1853 prints one *Tuum.* joined by a brace to both *Nomen* and *Regnum* — a single possessive governing two nouns. English must repeat it ("thy Name / thy Kingdom"), so the brace is kept in the transcript and the English repeats the possessive inside the brace marks to show where the join was. **This is a real loss, recorded here, not a translation choice.**

11. **The seven petitions keep Andrewes' numbering and his Latin word-order** — *Sanctificetur … Veniat … Fiat* put the verb first, so "Hallowed be / Come / Be done" rather than the BCP's "Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done." The numbering is why the Prayer is set out here at all; smoothing it into the received BCP order would erase the analysis.

12. **`Debita nostra` / `Debitoribus nostris` = debts / debtors**, following Matt. 6:12 as Andrewes' Latin does — deliberately not the BCP's "trespasses." The BCP governs this edition's register, not its textual base.

13. **`Sicut in cœlo sic et in terra`** kept in the Latin order (heaven first, then earth), against the BCP's "in earth, As it is in heaven."

14. **⚠ CROSS-REFERENCE, added at §40 (do not import).** "Who am I, O Lord, thy servant, and what is my house" (printed 349) **comes back at printed 384 as a Greek cue-line with an &c.** — "But who am I, O Lord?—&c." — closing §40's third catena. Andrewes expects the reader to finish it out of this page. **The English of the two is deliberately unequal:** §25 gives the sentence, §40 gives only the gesture. Do not lengthen §40's line to match this one, and do not shorten this one to match §40. See `40-aphormai-meditationum-english.md` flag 15.
