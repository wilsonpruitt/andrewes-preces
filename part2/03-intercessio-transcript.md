## Translator's flags

- **Section = printed 273–275(top).** The great bidding-prayer: *Pro Ecclesia / Pro Clero / Pro Populo / Pro Politiis / Pro Regno … Urbe … / Pro Rege / Pro Consiliariis / Judicibus / Exercitu / sobole / benefactoribus* — the classic Andrewes intercessory sweep (Church, Ministry, People, State, King, Councillors, Judges, Army, the rising generation, benefactors). Keep the *Pro …* headings as "For …" biddings.
- **Hebrew (274):** `הושיעה נא` (*Hoshia-na*, "Save now") glosses *O Domine, salvum fac*; `הצליחה נא` (*Hatzlicha-na*, "prosper now, send now prosperity") glosses *O Domine, prosperare* — both from Ps 118:25 (printed cxvii.25, LXX/Vulg. numbering; = the Hosanna). §11: keep the Hebrew, add translation.
- **Greek King-block (274):** `ὅπλῳ ἀληθείας καὶ εὐδοκίας στεφάνωσον αὐτὸν` = "with the shield of truth and good pleasure crown him" (echoes Ps 5:12 LXX *ὡς ὅπλῳ εὐδοκίας ἐστεφάνωσας ἡμᾶς*); `λάλησον τῇ καρδίᾳ αὐτοῦ ἀγαθά` = "speak good things to his heart"; `ὑπὲρ τῆς ἐκκλησίας καὶ τοῦ λαοῦ σου` = "for the sake of Thy Church and Thy people." This is a sustained Greek petition (main text) — translate it in full into the English line, not merely gloss.
- **Inline Greek (273):** `ὀρθοτομεῖν` (2 Tim 2:15, "rightly divide/handle the word") and `ὀρθοποδεῖν` (Gal 2:14, "walk uprightly/straight") — a rhyming *ortho-* pair braced to *ut recte secent / recto pede incedant* (the Latin already renders each). Keep the Greek apposed; the pun (cut-straight ‖ walk-straight) is the point.
- `Hostes { versutos detege / violentos contere }` (273): ordered brace — the *crafty* enemies expose, the *violent* crush. Preserve order.
- Psalm cribs (§9): Ps 79:13 ("render sevenfold into their bosom"), Ps 25:12 (Vulg./BCP "his seed shall inherit the land"), Ps 41:1 ("Blessed is he that considereth the poor and needy").
- `super egenum` / `egenos` and `res sacras` (274): benefactors shown either toward *sacred things* (the Church) or *the poor and needy* — the two objects of Christian benefaction.
- The numbered 1–8 (274–275) is a *Te rogamus, audi nos* petition-litany on behalf of benefactors and the departed; render "That Thou wouldest …" for each *Ut … [subjunctive]*.
- `lapsus carnis fragiles … recolas … labentes statumines` (275, item 3): "the frail fallings of the flesh regard with kind compassion, and stay up them that are slipping" — *statumines* = prop/shore up.
