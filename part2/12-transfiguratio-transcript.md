## Translator's flags

- **Section = printed 315(mid)–316(top).** *Transfiguratio* — the title here means Christ's **glorification / transfiguring in His exaltation**, the counterpart to §11's humiliation, NOT the Tabor Transfiguration. The eight items walk the whole triumph: (1) the triumph and leading-in-procession of the principalities and powers of darkness "in Himself" (*Col.* 2:15, "having spoiled principalities … He made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them"); (2) the mighty resurrection (*Phil.* 3:10, "the power of His resurrection"); (3) the **Apparitiones**; (4) the glorious Ascension (*Act.* 1:9); (5) the Session at the right hand (*Act.* 2:33); (6) the distribution of gifts (*Eph.* 4:8, "He gave gifts unto men"); (7) the continual intercession for us (*Heb.* 7:25, "He ever liveth to make intercession"); (8) the return to judgment (*Act.* 1:11, "shall so come in like manner"). Render "For the Transfiguring" if the governing *Pro* from §10–11 is felt to carry, but the section stands on its own numbered list.
- **The Apparitio brace (315–316):** the ten resurrection appearances in Andrewes' order — 1. to Magdalene (*Mar.* 16:9), 2. to the women (*Matt.* 28:9), 3. to Peter (*Luc.* 24:34), 4. to the two going toward Emmaus (*Vers.* 13—32), 5. to the Ten without Thomas (*Jo.* 20:20—24), 6. to the Eleven (*Vers.* 26, i.e. Thomas present), 7. at the sea of Tiberias (*Jo.* 21:1—24), 8. to James (1 *Cor.* 15:7), 9. to the Five hundred (*Vers.* 6), 10. at Bethany (*Luc.* 24:50, the Ascension scene). The list follows 1 Cor. 15 + the Gospel harmonies; keep the numbering. `Euntibus Emmauntem versus` = "to them going toward Emmaus" (*Emmauntem* is the edition's accusative form). `Decem sine Thoma` / `Undecim` = "the Ten without Thomas" / "the Eleven" (with Thomas) — the two upper-room appearances distinguished.
- **No Greek in this section** — pure Latin, a rare uninterrupted stretch in the "La+Gk" band; the map's flag is for the surrounding sections.
