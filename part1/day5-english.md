## Translator's flags

- **p.130 "THE THINGS OF THE ASCENSION"** (ΤΑ ΑΝΑΒΑΣΙΜΑ) — the day's heading for a catena of ascension-versicles (Ps 108:5, John 12:32, Col 3:2); the Fifth Day (Thursday) also commemorates the evening institution of "the wondrous Mystery of thy holy body and precious blood."
- **p.130 "As I live, saith Adonai the Lord"** (Ezek 33:11) — Andrewes' Greek transliterates the Hebrew divine name Ἀδωναί; kept "Adonai" per §11 (the English layer never silently drops a script).
- **p.132 the Daniel 9 penitence** (vv. 5–19) — a sustained cento in AV cadence. **"in our unfaithfulness, wherein thou hast rejected us"** (ἐν ἀθεσίᾳ ἡμῶν, ᾗ ἠθέτησας ἡμᾶς) — Andrewes' Greek makes God the subject of ἠθέτησας ("thou didst reject us"), where Dan 9:7 speaks of *our* trespass against him; followed the printed Greek and flagged.
- **p.134 "Now have I begun: this is the change of the right hand of the Most High"** (Ps 77:10) — Andrewes follows the LXX (νῦν ἠρξάμην, αὕτη ἡ ἀλλοίωσις τῆς δεξιᾶς τοῦ ὑψίστου), quite unlike the AV's "This is my infirmity: but I will remember the years of the right hand." Followed the Greek per §9.
- **p.134 the 1 John confession** — "if I say I have no sin…" (1:8), "faithful and just to forgive" (1:9), "an Advocate with the Father… the propitiation" (2:1–2), set within the questions of Ps 77 ("Will the Lord cast off for ever?").
- **p.136 the Beatitudes** (Matt 5:3–12) — laid out as a two-column table pairing the virtue-to-be with the blessing-to-receive (meek ↔ inherit the earth, hunger/thirst ↔ be filled, peaceable ↔ be called sons of God); the narrow columns wrap independently on the page. Framed by Heb 12:1 (ὄγκον … εὐπερίστατον ἁμαρτίαν) and Jas 1:21 ("superfluity of naughtiness").
- **p.136 the christological cento** — "He that cometh to God" (Heb 11:6) folded with "I know that my Redeemer liveth" (Job 19:25), Matt 16:16, John 4:42, 1 Tim 1:15 ("of whom I am chief"), and Acts 15:11.
- **p.138 "In the name { of the Father / of the Saviour, the Mediator / the Intercessor, the Redeemer / the twofold Paraclete } under the types of the Lamb and of the Dove"** — Andrewes' meditation on the divine names; the "twofold Paraclete" (διττός) is Christ the Advocate (1 John 2:1) and the Spirit the Comforter (John 14:16), figured by the Lamb and the Dove.
- **p.138–140 the Great Litany of Peace** (Ἐν εἰρήνῃ τοῦ Κυρίου δεηθῶμεν) — the Byzantine Ektene, rendered in its familiar liturgical English; closes with the Marian commemoration ("all-holy, undefiled, more than blessed, Mother of God and ever-virgin Mary"), the grace (2 Cor 13:14), and the Jude doxology (24–25).
- **p.142–144 the thanksgiving** — a stack of braced triads (being/life/reason; nurture/keeping/governance; …); **μακρομακροθυμία** "long-longsuffering" is Andrewes' coined intensive; the three vertical braces join a present with a future verb (I confess / and will confess; bless; give thanks — now and always). Closes with "who am I… a dead dog such as I am" (2 Sam 7:18; 9:8) and "what shall I render" (Ps 116:12).
- **p.146 "hath spared me / hath waited for me"** (ἐφείσατό μου / ἀνέμενέ με) — the Latin renders ἀνέμενε as *toleravit* ("bore with me"); the Greek ἀναμένω is "await / wait for," kept as "waited for me." The day closes with the Sanctus and the doxology of Rev 4:8, 11.

### Transcription flags carried from `day5-transcript.md`

- **p.132 ᾗ ἠθέτησας ἡμᾶς** — the second-singular verb ("thou didst reject us") is unusual against Dan 9:7 but is clearly what the 1853 prints; see the translation flag above.
- **Latin p.145 "Reprehensionibus"** — the 1853 prints what reads as "Reprehensiouibus" (a turned n / u); read as *Reprehensionibus* (= Greek ταῖς ἐπιτιμήσεσι, "by their rebukes").
- **Latin p.133 "Dan. ix. 5"** — printed without a closing period after the verse number; left verbatim.
