# Part I front matter — ΕΥΧΑΙ ΚΑΘΗΜΕΡΙΝΑΙ / PRECES QUOTIDIANÆ (before Day 1) — English (Wroot Press translation)

Translated from the Greek of the 1853 text (`front-transcript.md`), line-keyed page by page to the Greek leaves; the Latin recto consulted as first witness. Register per `CONVENTIONS.md` §8–10. Scripture refs stay on the Latin layer; divergences and the grecized place-names are flagged at the end. This is the material that precedes Day 1: the half-title, the Hours and Places of Prayer, the Intercessions, the Confessions, the Introit, and the Morning Office.

## Translator's flags

- **printed 2 (Dan. 6:10).** Greek *ἐξομολογούμενος* — rendered "giving thanks" with AV (Dan. 6:10 "gave thanks"); the verb equally bears "making confession," which suits the prayer-hours frame. Andrewes' Ps. 55:17 (*διηγήσομαι… ἀπαγγελῶ*, LXX) means "declare… make known," not AV's "pray… cry aloud"; the Greek is followed.
- **printed 4 (Ps. 99:6).** *Ἐν δὲ λιταζομένοισι Σαμουήλ* — the dative uses the Homeric/epic ‑οισι ending; = "and Samuel among them that make supplication." The elevated form is Andrewes', not a corruption.
- **printed 6 (Ps. 65:2–3).** *λόγοι ἀνομιῶν ὑπερεδυνάμωσαν* = "words of iniquities have prevailed" (LXX); AV reads simply "iniquities prevail." The Greek is followed. *φροντιστήριον* here = "the holy college/foundation" (Andrewes' word for his own house of learning); recurs in the Introit and in Day 7.
- **printed 10 (Ps. 76:10).** Whole verse follows the LXX: *ἐνθύμιον* = "the thought (of man)," and *ἑορτάσει* = "shall keep holy day"; AV (from the Hebrew) reads "the wrath of man shall praise thee… the remainder of wrath shalt thou restrain." Andrewes' departure from the AV text is the point. The clipped brace-cues (*κατά / διά / ὥς* = "according to / for and for the sake of / even as") are Andrewes' shorthand petitions; kept terse as printed.
- **printed 12 (the Introit catalogue).** Andrewes grecizes his own biography as a cipher of place-names — the same list that returns in Day 7's ΑΓΑΘΥΝΟΝ intercession. Rendered here by the Greek sense; the identifications: *Φροντιστήριον* = the College (Pembroke); *Πηγὴ Μεσημβρινή* ("Southern Spring") = Southwell; *Ἐπιζεφύριον* ("the Western Minster") = Westminster; *Κικεστρία* = Chichester; *Ἐλεόπολις* ("City of Mercy") = Ely; *Διοίκησις Οὔιντον* = the diocese of Winchester (Winton); *Τέμενος Βασιλικόν* = the Chapel Royal; *Οἰκονομία ἐλεημοσύνης Βασιλικῆς* = the Royal Almonry. "Of old / and now" (*Τῶν πάλαι / Τῶν νῦν*) divides the sees he once held from those he held when he wrote.
- **printed 16 (the Morning Office).** *Παράσχου, Κύριε* is the Byzantine litany response, "Grant, O Lord"; kept as the refrain. In the scan the first response's glyph reads "Κίριε," a worn Υ — it is *Κύριε*.
- **printed 18 (ΟΥΣΙΑ ὑπερούσιε).** "O Being beyond all being, nature uncreated" — the Dionysian *hyperousios*; a deliberately philosophical opening to a morning prayer. *τῷ πάνυ* ("the utter [sinner]") sharpens the Publican's *ἁμαρτωλῷ* toward 1 Tim. 1:15's "chief of sinners."
- **printed 20.** *Τὴν ἐπωνυμίαν σὴν* = "thy surname / the name of thine own set upon me" (i.e. *Christian*, the name of Christ borne by the baptized); rendered "the surname of thine own upon me."
- **printed 26 (1 Sam. 25:31).** *εἰς λυγμὸν* (LXX) is literally "a sobbing/catching of the breath in grief" — AV "grief"; rendered "a sobbing of grief" to keep the physical image. *ἐν ἀκάνθαις φράξον τὰ ὦτά* ("hedge up mine ears with thorns," cf. Sir. 28:24) is Andrewes' image, not a canonical verse.
- **General.** Only the Greek leaves (even printed pages) carry an English block, since Part I translates the Greek; the Latin recto was read as construal-witness throughout and agreed except where flagged. Divine-name emphasis (§10) is applied where the Greek itself sets a word in capitals (section-openers); *Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ* at printed 18 is in ordinary case in the 1853 text and is rendered "Jesus Christ."
