## Translator's flags

- **p.92 "as in a wineskin"** (Ps 33:7) — Andrewes follows the LXX ὡς ἐν ἀσκῷ ("as in a wineskin/bottle"); AV renders the Hebrew "as an heap." Followed the Greek per §9.
- **p.94 "strange sins"** (Ps 19:13) — Andrewes' LXX has ἀπὸ ἀλλοτρίων ("from strangers/alien [sins]"); the AV famously reads "presumptuous sins." Followed the Greek (ἀλλοτρίων = strange/alien); φείσαι rendered "spare … from."
- **p.94 "vapour of smoke"** (Joel 2:30) — AV Joel reads "pillars of smoke"; Andrewes' Greek ἀτμὶς καπνοῦ = "vapour of smoke" (so LXX / Acts 2:19 AV). Followed the Greek.
- **p.96 double brace [into]** — 2 Cor. vii. 11, the seven fruits of godly sorrow. The RIGHT column echoes AV wording (carefulness, clearing of yourselves, indignation, fear, vehement desire, zeal, revenge); the LEFT column names the sins turned from (Fancy, Fault, Guilt/offence, Sin, Transgression, Unrighteousness, Abomination). The whole rides on "Turn my mourning into joy" (Ps 30:11).
- **p.98 "{ a calling-out from the / a hallowing in the } Universal"** — this is the creed's "holy catholic Church": Ἔκκλησις ἐκ τοῦ … / ἁγιασμὸς ἐν τῷ … καθόλου (Lat. Evocatio ex / Sanctificatio in / Universo). Rendered to keep the ec-clesia / sanctification wordplay and καθόλου = "Universal / catholic."
- **p.100 intercession list** — this two-column block is elliptical prayer-shorthand (bare nouns, objects of an implied "remember / keep / give"); the English preserves the list quality rather than supplying verbs. Where the Latin glosses rather than matches, the English follows the Greek: ὑγιείαν "health" (Lat. Medicinam), βιωτικὰ "the things of this life" (Lat. Necessaria), ἐπιτροπὴ "a trust committed" (Lat. Fidei commissio), ἀσχολία "occupation" (Lat. Impediri), πανένδεια "utter want" (Lat. Destitutos). Several renderings here are interpretive.
- **p.102 AV echoes** — φιλανθρωπία "love toward man" (Titus 3:4 AV), ἐπιείκεια "gentleness" (2 Cor. 10:1 AV), ὑπεριδὼν "winked at" (Acts 17:30 AV). The whole ΑΓΑΘΩΣΥΝΗ catalogue is a cento of the mercy-vocabulary of Scripture.

### Transcription flags carried from `day3-transcript.md`

- **Right-column editorial braces (Latin p.101).** The Latin prints braces joining three right-column pairs — { Animabus / Corporibus }, { olim / in præsens }, { Destitutos / In extremis } esse — that the Greek image (p.100) shows plain (ψυχὰς/σώματα, τὸ πάλαι/τὸ παρὸν, πανένδεια/τὰ ἔσχατα). Transcribed each side as printed; verify against a cleaner scan whether the Greek braces simply failed to print.
- **ἐπιτροπὴ (Greek p.100)** — accent/ending unclear in the scan (ή vs ὴ; possible final ν); the Latin "Fidei commissio" supports nom. sing.
- **Ps ref (Latin p.101)** printed "cxxi. [5.]" with a stray extra open-bracket; normalized to [Ps. cxxi. 5.].
- **Mansuetudo / Lenitas (Latin p.103)** are braced to a single ref (2 Cor. x. 1); the Greek πρᾳότης / ἐπιείκεια are printed plain.
