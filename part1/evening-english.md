# Part I — The Evening Office (Ἡ ΔΕΙΛΙΝΗ ΑΚΟΛΟΥΘΙΑ / OFFICIUM VESPERTINUM) — English (Wroot Press translation)

Translated from the Greek of the 1853 text (`evening-transcript.md`), line-keyed page by page to the Greek leaves (even printed pages 220–250); the Latin recto consulted as first witness. Register per `CONVENTIONS.md` §8–10 — with §9's Psalter rule now in force: the psalm-lines are crib-checked against the **Coverdale/BCP Psalter** ahead of the AV, and the LXX-vs-AV departures are flagged at the end. The office runs: the evening remembrance of mortality, the Blessing (*Εὐλογητὸς εἶ*), the Confession at evening, the Prayer at evening (compline petitions), the great Intercession catalogue, the communion devotions **Before** and **After** Communion (woven from the Divine Liturgy), and the closing catechism from Luke 3.

## Translator's flags

- **The Psalter crib (new CONVENTIONS §9).** The Evening Office is dense with psalmody, and it is the first section translated under the rule that reaches first for the **Coverdale/BCP Psalter** ahead of the AV. Two lines show *why* the rule earns its place — the BCP stands **with Andrewes against the AV** because Coverdale, like Andrewes, worked through the Latin/LXX:
  - **printed 226, Ps 51:1.** Andrewes' *κατὰ τὸ μέγα ἔλεός σου … ἐξάλειψον τὸ ἀνόμημά μου* renders as BCP "after thy great **mercy** … blot out mine **offence**" (singular), not AV "according to thy **lovingkindness** … blot out my **transgressions**." Followed BCP.
  - **printed 228, Ps 13:3.** *ἵνα μὴ ὑπνώσω εἰς θάνατον* = BCP "that I sleep not in death" verbatim (AV "lest I sleep the sleep of death").
- **LXX-vs-AV departures (follow Andrewes, per §9).** Andrewes quotes the Septuagint Psalter, which diverges from the Hebrew the AV renders. Kept the LXX reading each time:
  - **printed 226, Ps 19:13.** *ἀπὸ τῶν ἀλλοτρίων φεῖσαι τοῦ δούλου σου* (Lat. *ab alienis*) = "from other men's sins spare thy servant" — LXX *ἀλλοτρίων*, "of strangers/others," where AV **and** Coverdale both read "presumptuous sins" (Heb. *zēdîm*). Here Andrewes is alone with the LXX/Vulgate; cf. 1 Tim 5:22, "partaker of other men's sins."
  - **printed 230, Ps 38:7.** *Μὴ πλησθῶσιν αἱ ψόαι τῶν ἐμπαιγμάτων* = "let not my loins be filled with **illusions**" — LXX *ἐμπαιγμῶν* (mockeries/night-illusions, apt at compline), where AV has "a loathsome disease." Latin *illusionibus* confirms.
  - **printed 230, Ps 16:7 (BCP 16:8).** *παιδεύωσί με οἱ νεφροί μου* rendered "let my reins **chasten** me" with the BCP verb (AV "instruct").
  - **printed 228, Ps 91:6.** *ἀπὸ πράγματος ἐν σκότει διαπορευομένου* = "from the **thing** that walketh through in darkness" — LXX *πράγματος* (Ps 90:6), where AV/BCP read "pestilence."
  - **printed 234, Ps 4:8.** *ἐπ' ἐλπίδι κατοικίσεις με* = "shalt make me dwell in **hope**" — LXX *ἐπ' ἐλπίδι*, where both AV and BCP read "in **safety**" (Heb. *lābeṭaḥ*). Andrewes again with the LXX alone. *ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτὸ* (LXX "all at once / together") is carried by the AV's "**both** lay me down and sleep."
- **Non-Psalter LXX/Vulgate readings.** **printed 224, Prov 24:16** — Andrewes writes *Ἑπτάκις ἡμέρας πεσεῖται ὁ δίκαιος*, adding *ἡμέρας* ("**a day**") to the LXX, a conflation with Ps 119:164 ("seven times a day do I praise thee"); rendered "seven times a day." **printed 228, Hos 13:9** — *ἡ διαφθορά μου ἐμοὶ ἐστὶ παρ' ἐμοῦ* = "my destruction is of myself, from me" (LXX/Vulg *perditio mea a me*), where AV reads "O Israel, thou hast destroyed thyself."
- **Liturgical provenance (printed 238–248).** The communion devotions are a cento of the Byzantine Divine Liturgy (Chrysostom/Basil): the elevation prayer *Πρόσχες, Κύριε* ("Attend, O Lord … come to sanctify us"), the anamnesis *Μεμνημένοι οὖν, Δέσποτα* ("Remembering therefore, O Master"), the *Ἀμνὲ Θεοῦ* (Agnus Dei, John 1:29), and the post-communion thanksgiving *Ἤνυσται καὶ τετέλεσται* ("It is accomplished and finished"). These are rendered in settled liturgical-English cadence, not fresh-minted. **printed 238** — the opening *Οὐκ ἄξιός εἰμι … ὑπὸ τὴν … στέγην … εἰσέλθῃς* is Matt 8:8 (the centurion), the same verse behind the BCP **Prayer of Humble Access**; Andrewes' Greek is set in that key ("I am not worthy … under the roof of the house of my soul").
- **Greek-vs-Latin divergences (Part I rule: follow the Greek, note the Latin).**
  - **printed 232.** The estates of power run **four-fold** in the Greek — *Βουλὴν, Δίκην, Κράτος, Βίαν* ("Council, Justice, Might, Force") — where the Latin collapses to three (*Consiliarios, Judices, Vim bellicam*). Kept the Greek four.
  - **printed 234.** *σκάνδαλον δοθὲν* = "an offence **given**"; the Latin recto reads *Scandalum a me accepisse*, "an offence **taken** from me." Followed the Greek.
  - **printed 240.** *νῆψιν παντὸς καρποῦ πνευματικοῦ* = "a sober **watch** over every spiritual fruit" (*νῆψις*, soberness); Latin *Excitationem* ("a stirring-up"). Greek kept; likewise **printed 242** *νῆψιν εὐχαριστίας* "a soberness of thanksgiving" (Lat. *Expergefactionem*, "an awakening").
  - **printed 246.** *τῆς φαύλης μου συνειδήσεως* = "my evil **conscience**"; Latin *malæ meæ conversationis*, "my evil **conversation** [manner of life]." Followed the Greek.
- **printed 242, *λόγον ἱκανὸν λογίας* (1 Cor 16:1).** *λογία* is the technical NT word for the church **collection**/alms; "a fit reckoning of the collection" — i.e. the grace to give rightly. The Latin *Rationem idoneam collectæ* confirms the sense.
- **printed 242, *ἐφόδιον ζωῆς Πνευματικῆς*.** Rendered with the precise sacramental term "**viaticum**" (Lat. *Viaticum vitæ spiritualis*) — provision for the journey — since the whole passage is a communion prayer.
- **printed 244, the Session.** *τῆς ἐκ δεξιῶν σου τοῦ Πατρὸς καθέδρας* — the prayer addresses the Father (*Δέσποτα*), so *σου τοῦ Πατρὸς* is vocative-in-apposition: "the Sitting at **thy** right hand, O Father" (the Son's session). The Latin makes it third-person, *a dextra Dei Patris*. The following *αὐτοῦ παρουσίας* then shifts to "**his** coming" (the Son's Advent).
- **printed 238/239 (source defect, not a translation issue).** Only the Greek verso (238) carries an English block, as throughout; the Latin recto (239) is the leaf cut off in the photographed-spread scan (see the transcript and STRUCTURE.md). The English layer is unaffected — but the Latin construal-witness for 239 is missing until a clean scan is obtained.
- **General.** Only the Greek leaves (even printed pages) carry an English block; the Latin recto is the construal-witness throughout. Section-openers set in Greek capitals keep their emphasis in caps (O LORD, TO me, IT is, THE Lord, WHAT). Bracketed section titles ([INTERCESSION], [AT THE HOLY EUCHARIST], the numbered communion rubrics) reproduce the 1853 editor's bracketed supplies.
