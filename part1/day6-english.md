## Translator's flags

- **p.148 the three-column anthropology table** — Andrewes pairs the members of the body (heart, reins, eyes, ears, tongue, hands, feet) with the faculties (life, sense, reason, spirit, free-will, memory, conscience) and the means of the knowledge of God — "that which may be known of God" (τὸ γνωστὸν τοῦ Θεοῦ, Rom 1:19), the written Law, the oracles of the Prophets, the melody of Psalms, the admonition of Proverbs, the experience of histories, the worship of sacrifices.
- **p.150 "the life-giving seed"** (Gen 3:15) — the Sixth Day (Friday) commemorates the Passion; hence "the holy sufferings of this day" and the eucharistic "O by thy saving sufferings, save us."
- **p.150–152 the Minor-Prophets penitence cento** — a sustained chain of the twelve: Amos 7 ("who shall raise up Jacob? for he is small"), Jonah 2 ("they that observe lying vanities forsake their own mercy"; "when my soul fainted within me, I remembered the Lord"), Micah 7 ("who is a God like unto thee, that passeth by iniquity … cast all our sins into the depths of the sea"), Habakkuk 3 ("I have heard thy speech and was afraid … in wrath remember mercy"), Zechariah 3 ("clothed with filthy garments … the devil standing at my right hand") and 9/13 ("the blood of thy covenant … the fountain opened for uncleanness").
- **p.154 the Passion catalogue** (Διὰ / Per) — one great brace naming the sufferings of the Cross (the bloody sweat in clots, the crown of thorns, vinegar and gall, the pierced hands and feet, the strong cry Ἠλὶ Ἠλί, the spear, the water and blood), framed by the three dominical words: "Father, forgive" (Luke 23:34), "Remember me" (23:42), "lay not this sin" (Acts 7:60).
- **p.156–158 the Galatians and Isaiah catalogues** — the works of the flesh (Gal 5:19–21) and the fruits of the Spirit (Gal 5:22–23) as facing tables; the sevenfold Spirit of Isa 11:2 (wisdom, understanding, counsel, might, knowledge, godliness, fear of the Lord); the gifts of 1 Cor 12:8–10 (word of wisdom/knowledge, faith, healings, miracles, prophecy, discerning of spirits, kinds and interpretation of tongues).
- **p.158–159 the ΠΙΣΤΕΥΩ / CREDO benefit-litany** — a chain of "because thou didst X, let not Y perish" (created me → the work of thy hands despise not; redeemed me → the price of the ransom lose not; sanctified me → thy hallowing destroy not), closing "grafted into the good olive tree (Rom 11:24), a member of the mystical body (Eph 5:30) — cut me not off."
- **p.160–161 the intercession** (the Ὑπὲρ / Pro litany) — the Byzantine augmented litany: "the Christ-loving army against the enemies of our most holy faith," the founders of "this holy dwelling," and the commemoration of the departed.
- **p.162–163 the "of Christ … me" brace prayer** — the *Anima Christi* tradition cast as a triple brace: soul, body, blood, water, stripe, sweat, and wound *of Christ* — hallow, strengthen, redeem, wash, heal, refresh, hide *me*; sealed with the peace of God that passeth all understanding (Phil 4:7).
- **p.164–167 the doxology of the Name and of the Lamb** — the incarnation-recital (Gal 4:4; Heb 2:16 — the obedience fulfilled, the curse borne, the world redeemed and quickened), the witness of the martyrs and "the most wondrous conversion of the whole world … without power, persuasion, or force," resolving into the sevenfold acclamation of the Name (blessed, praised, hymned, magnified, exalted, glorified, hallowed) and the Revelation doxologies (Rev 5:9, 12, 13; 7:10, 12).
