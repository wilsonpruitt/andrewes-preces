OF THE FIRST DAY.

THROUGH the tender mercies
        of our God, the Dayspring
        from on high hath visited us.
Glory to thee, O Lord, glory to thee,
    who didst create the light, and enlighten
        the world.
    Light visible,
        the sun's ray, the flame of fire,
            day and night,
            evening and morning.
    Light intelligible,
        that which may be known of God,
            that which is written of the Law,
                the oracles of Prophets,
                the melody of Psalms,
                the admonition of Proverbs,
                the experience of Histories,
    Light that knows no evening.

God is the Lord, who hath shewed us light,
    Keep ye the feast with thick boughs,
        even unto the horns of the altar.
By thine own resurrection raise us up
        unto newness of life,

The God of peace, who brought again from the dead

    to do his will,
Working in us that which is wellpleasing in his sight,
    through JESUS CHRIST,
        to whom be glory for ever.
Thou who thy thrice-holy Spirit upon this day
    didst send down upon thy disciples,
    Take not the same, O Lord, from us,
    But renew it day by day
        in us who beseech thee.

## Translator's flags (for the unit files' Notes)

- **p. 34 line 1:** Greek Ἤτασάς με ὧν ἥμαρτον ("thou hast chastened/tried me for what I sinned") vs Latin *Non recepi* (Job xxxiii. 27, "it profited me not") — a real Gr/La divergence; English follows the Greek.
- **p. 34:** Greek Καὶ ἐγὼ ἥμαρτον ("and I have sinned") where Neh. ix. 33 AV has "but we have done wickedly" — followed Andrewes' Greek singular.
- **p. 38:** Ἀπὸ τῶν ἀλλοτρίων (Ps. xix. 13 LXX "strange sins") vs AV "presumptuous sins" — followed the Greek.
- **p. 40:** ἀσέβειαν τῆς καρδίας (Ps. xxxii LXX) vs AV "iniquity of my sin" — followed the Greek.
- **p. 54:** Ἡ ὑπομονὴ τῶν νήσων — "patience/stay of the isles" (Isa. li. 5); "deliver this island" = Andrewes praying for England; kept concrete.
- **p. 56:** θεοφυλάκτου Βασιλέως — "our King, whom God doth guard" (Byzantine liturgical epithet).
- **p. 66:** ἐνεργέτῃ (Latin *Auctori*) — rendered "worker"; if the print intends εὐεργέτῃ, "benefactor" — check against Variae Lectiones when transcribing them.
- **p. 66:** ὥρᾳ καὶ χώρᾳ — Andrewes' jingle; rendered "in each season, in each region" to keep the pairing audible (Latin [Hora et regione] is bracketed as the 1853 editor's supplement).
- **Print-unclear spots** carried from the transcript: Κύριε/Κίριε (p. 40), ζῶν/ζαν (p. 40), χεῖράς/χεῖρές (p. 40), ἁμαρτίαν breathing (p. 46), ἁγίου/ἁγίοι (p. 44).
- **Ref errors as printed** (do not silently fix): *Psal.* cxxx. 30 (should be cxxx. 3), *Psal.* xxxv. 3 for Dic animæ meæ (correct), *Galat.* vi 1 missing period.
