## Translator's flags

- **printed 70, "IN the morning shalt thou hear my voice…"** — Ps 5:3. Andrewes' Greek (ἐπόψει με, "thou shalt look upon me") follows the LXX cast into the second person, not AV's "will I direct my prayer… and will look up." English follows the Greek.
- **printed 70, "walked contrary before thee"** (ἐναντίον σου πλάγιος) — Lev 26:40. πλάγιος is literally "crookedly/perversely"; rendered "contrary" to echo AV (Latin *ex adverso tibi*).
- **printed 72, "my whole life in the light of thy countenance"** (τὸν αἰῶνά μου εἰς φωτισμὸν τοῦ προσώπου σου) — Ps 90:8 LXX (89:8), "our age/lifetime in the light of thy countenance." AV renders the Hebrew differently ("our secret sins in the light of thy countenance"); English follows Andrewes' Greek/LXX (Latin *sæculum meum*).
- **printed 72, "as an adversary against thee"** (κατεντευκτήν σοι) — Job 7:20. The LXX word κατεντευκτής means "one who pleads/accuses against"; AV renders the Hebrew "as a mark against thee." English follows the Greek sense, guided by the Latin *contrarium tibi* ("as one opposed to thee").
- **printed 72, "that it go not down into corruption"** (εἰς διαφθοράν) — Job 33:24 / echo of Ps 16:10 ("see corruption"). Latin *interitum* ("destruction"). The Greek word is broken across two printed lines (δια-/φθοράν); the English mirrors the break (cor-/ruption).
- **printed 78, "And now, what is my hope? … Is it not thou, O Lord?"** (τίς ὑπομονή μου; οὐχί σύ, Κύριε;) — Ps 39:7 LXX (38:8); the BCP cadence ("And now, Lord, what is my hope: truly my hope is even in thee") is echoed, but Andrewes' Greek has no vocative in the first line.
- **printed 82, "brethren, sisters"** (ἀδελφῶν, ἀδελφίδων) — the Greek ἀδελφίδων strictly means "nephews/nieces" (sisters' children); the Latin gives *Sororibus* ("sisters"), and the natural pairing with ἀδελφῶν ("brothers") supports "sisters." Rendered "sisters" per the Latin and the evident pairing.
- **printed 82, place-names** — τῆς Πηγῆς Μεσημβρινῆς / τῆς τοῦ Ἐπιζεφυρίου Μοναστηρίου are Grecized English place-names; the Latin glosses them *sc. Southwell* and *sc. Westmonasterii* (Westminster). English keeps the descriptive form ("the Southern Spring," "the Western Monastery") to match the Greek; the identifications sit on the Latin layer.
- **printed 84, "the Helps, the Governments"** (Ἀντιλήψεων, Κυβερνήσεων) — 1 Cor 12:28; AV wording preserved.
- **printed 84, dioceses** (Κικεστρίας, Ἐλεοπόλεως, τῆσδε καὶ νῦν) — Chichester, Ely, and "this present see" (Winchester, per the Latin gloss *sc. Winton*) — Andrewes' own successive sees.
- **printed 88, "The Lord, the Lord… Merciful and Gracious…"** — Exod 34:6–7, the proclamation of the Name; AV wording ("keeping mercy for thousands," "will by no means clear the guilty," "visiting… the fathers upon the children") echoed throughout the numbered epithets.
- **printed 88, "Glory to GOD in the highest"** (Δόξα ἐν ὑψίστοις ΘΕΩ) — Luke 2:14; ΘΕΩ set in capitals in the Greek, so GOD is capitalised per §10.
- **printed 90 (transcript), "Dominatioesn"** — the 1853 Latin misprints *Dominationes* (Greek Κυριότητες, "Dominions"); transcribed verbatim with a note, translated as "Dominions."
- **Transcription-unclear glyphs (see transcript inline comments):** printed 70 ὑπερίδόν σε (ambiguous ὑπερίδόν/ὑπερεῖδόν — "disregarded thee," Latin *neglexi te*); printed 74 the numerals θ΄ (9) and ι΄ (10) in the exorcism list, whose glyphs are ambiguous but fixed by the one-to-one Latin numbering on p.75; printed 74/76 φιλοφροσύνην, completed across a faint scan-edge.
- **Nested braces, printed 80–81** — the Greek and Latin diverge in their bracketing of the "help/comfort" and "thanksgiving/sobriety" petitions and the four churches; each side is transcribed as printed, and the English is keyed to the Greek. See the transcript's inline comments.
