# Day 7 — OF THE SEVENTH DAY — English (Wroot Press translation)

Translated from the Greek of the 1853 text (`day7-transcript.md`), line-keyed page by page; Latin consulted as first witness. Register per `CONVENTIONS.md` §8–10. Scripture refs stay on the Latin layer; divergences flagged at the end. Day 7 is the last of the seven days.

## Translator's flags (for the unit files' Notes)

- **p. 168:** `ἑβδόμῃ … αὐτήν` — "the seventh [day] … it" (Gen. ii. 2–3); the feminine pronoun follows the feminine ἡμέρᾳ. `ἀναπαύσεως ἀμοιβαίας` rendered "the rest that is our recompense" (the reciprocal/answering rest); `τῶν νεκρωσίμων τοῦ Χριστοῦ` = "the dying of Christ" (2 Cor. iv. 10); `προαναπαυσαμένων` = "them that have fallen asleep before us" (the departed).
- **p. 170:** Prayer of Manasseh cento (so the Latin `[Orat. Manassis.]`); followed the deuterocanonical wording. `ἄνες μοι` = "forgive me" (release/remit); `οὐκ ἔστι μοι ἄνεσις` = "I have no relief."
- **p. 172:** Gospel-cries litany; echoed the AV where the Greek matches (Matt. viii, ix; Mark iv, vii, x; Luke xiii, xvii; John v; Ps. xxxv, lxxix; 2 Cor. xii). The sins-catalogue words are near-synonyms; differentiated so each column reads distinctly (failings/shortcomings/slips/stumblings/trespasses/offences/transgressions | debts/sins/faults/ignorances/iniquities/impieties/wrongs/pollutions).
- **p. 174:** The three verb-catalogue headers `Τὸ ὑπεύθυνον / Τὸ ῥυπαρὸν / Τὸ σύντριμμα` = "The debt / The defilement / The brokenness" (Latin *Reatus / Macula / Languor*). `Ἐπιχορηγῆσαι … ἐν τῇ …` is the 2 Pet. i. 5–7 virtue chain — "supply *in* [thy] faith virtue," etc.; kept the "in thy {" cue with the dative→accusative pairing. Closing prose = 2 Pet. i. 9–10 (calling and election sure).
- **p. 176:** Greek prints `ἔδως` where the Latin *dedisti* and the sense require `ἔδωκας` ("who gavest me a living soul," Gen. ii. 7) — translated as "gavest." `ἐν τοῖς ἁγιάσμασιν αὐτοῦ` (Latin *in Sacramentis Ipsius*) rendered "in his holy things" (sanctuaries/sacraments). The `μόνον` grace-in-vain clause runs across into p. 178.
- **p. 178:** `τὴν ἁγίαν μονὴν ταύτην` = "this holy house/dwelling"; the Latin renders *Collegium* (Andrewes' college) — kept the generic "house." `ΕΠΟΥΡΑΝΙΕ Βασιλεῦ` = "O Heavenly King" (opening of the Byzantine invocation of the Spirit).
- **p. 180:** `πρόφθασον` = "prevent" in the BCP sense (go before/forestall). `τοῦ αἰῶνος τῶν αἰώνων` kept literal "the age of ages" (Eph. iii. 21).
- **p. 182:** `ὁ Βασιλεὺς τῶν ἐθνῶν ἁγίων` = "King of the holy nations" — AV Rev. xv. 3 reads "King of saints," other MSS "King of the nations/ages"; followed Andrewes' Greek. `μόνος ἅγιος ὅσιος` doubles holy/gracious (AV "thou only art holy") — rendered "holy and gracious."
- **p. 184:** `οὔτε κραυγὴ` = "neither crying" (Rev. xxi. 4); Andrewes' Greek has only κραυγή + πόνος, where the AV pairs sorrow + crying.
- **pp. 186–188:** The great personal intercession — Andrewes grecizes his own English places; translated to the real referents and flagged: `τῶν Παναγίων Βερκίγγ` = **All-Hallows Barking** (his baptism church); `τοῦ ἁγίου Αἰγιδίου` = **St Giles** (Cripplegate, his cure); `Πεμβροχαύλην` = **Pembroke Hall**; `τῆς Πηγῆς Μεσημβρινῆς` = **Southwell** ("the southern well"); `τοῦ Ἐπιζεφυρίου Μοναστηρίου` = **Westminster** ("the western monastery"); the three sees `Κικεστρίας / Ἐλεοπόλεως / τῶν Σιμήνων Οὐιντονίας` = **Chichester / Ely / Winchester**. `τὸ συνέδριον` (Latin *Collegium*) rendered "the college." `τὴν καθ' ὅλου Ἐκκλησίαν` = "the Church universal" (Latin *Catholicam*).
- **p. 190:** `Σοὶ πρέπει ὕμνος` rendered with the AV of Ps. lxv. 1 ("Praise waiteth for thee, O God"). `ἥξει καὶ ἥδε` = "this soul also shall come" (feminine ἥδε = ἡ ψυχή). `εὖ ἔδρασε τὴν κτίσιν` = "hath done good unto all the creation" (accusative of the thing benefited, per the Latin *benefecit Creaturæ*).
- **p. 192:** `Θέσο` (unusual form) read as thanksgiving, "Thou settest my soul in life" (Ps. lxvi. 9); the aorist participles `Ῥύσας / Μὴ παραδοὺς` rendered "who hast delivered / who hast not given me over." Isa. xxxviii. 12 weaver-image.
- **p. 196:** closing doxology; `Παναγίῳ σου ὀνόματι` = "thy all-holy Name"; kept the ascending run of "glory and praise and blessing and thanksgiving … unto the ages of ages."
