# *Variæ Lectiones et Addenda Quædam* — raw transcript

Source: 1853 Parker ed., the apparatus following the primary text — **PDF 482–491**, images `raw/end/p-NNN.jpg`. The apparatus is **separately foliated in lower-case roman**: PDF 482 = **[i]** (unnumbered, the title page), 483 = **ii**, 484 = **iii**, and so on to PDF 491 = **x**. ⚠ **Do not address these pages by their PDF number in any ledger** — the apparatus's own `p. N. lin. N.` citations refer to **printed arabic pages of the primary text**, and confusing the two numbering systems would wreck the collation.

**This is a collation ledger, not primary text.** It is transcribed verbatim and it is NOT translated: the entries are the 1853 editor's Latin notes about a manuscript, addressed to a reader who has the book open. Class-A readings are resolved against our own files in `CLASS-A-ledger.md`; **nothing here is silently applied to any transcript.**

Transcription discipline: verbatim incl. accentuation; Greek per the standing rule (vision only, `[?]` where unreadable, **never inferred from context**); Hebrew given as printed, right-to-left, with a confidence note wherever the 200-dpi image cannot separate look-alike sorts (ד/ר, ב/כ); the editor's own italics preserved; `lin.` / `col.` / `sqq.` / `ult.` / `penult.` left unexpanded.

⚠ **THE DOT-MARKS.** The apograph indexes certain prayers with **marks made of dots** — one dot, two dots, three (∴), four (⸬), and apparently more. Where the count is legible it is given as that many `·` inside braces, e.g. `{···}`. **Where the arrangement cannot be resolved at 200 dpi it is given as `{·?}` and must not be guessed** — these marks are the manuscript's own index to the prayers and an invented arrangement would corrupt it. **NEW WATCH ITEM; see the notes at the foot of this file.**

  i. Hoc apographum Græca tantum continet usque ad pag. 250
        nostræ editionis, sine versione Latina, quam Editor ipse
        confecisse videtur.

        ἐν ἐπιγείοις, p. 214.  εἴσοδος, p. 10, *erratum* videtur.
iii. Græcis literis, α΄, β΄, γ΄, (κ.τ.λ.) non Arabicis, notantur partes
distinctæ.
 iv. Dies sua quisque nota astronomica distinguitur.
    Ea quæ in apographo a nostra editione differunt, in tres classes
videntur distinguenda, quas literis A, B, C, indicavimus.

    A.  Variæ Lectiones quæ in textu ipso occurrunt, et idcirco pro
veris sunt habendæ.

    B.  Intervalla sive spatia, quæ mentem auctoris sæpius indicant.

    C.  Variæ Lectiones quas Ricardus Drake in ora libri exaravit,
eo fere animo ut loca e Sacris Scripturis citata ad textum receptum
propius accederent.
