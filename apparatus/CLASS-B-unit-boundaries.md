# Class B — the spacing record against our unit boundaries

Class B is *"Intervalla sive spatia, **quæ mentem auctoris sæpius indicant**"* — the intervals and spaces, **which often show the author's mind**. It is the manuscript's own record of where Andrewes left air on the page, and it is the highest-value class in the apparatus for the edition's *structure*, as CONVENTIONS §13 anticipated.

**Scope: printed pp. 2–250 only** — the whole extent of the apograph.

## ⚠ First, the negative result the brief asked for

The brief asked that class B be weighed against **the boundaries we chose ourselves** — naming **§28's** and **Part III's §2/§3 cut**.

**Class B cannot speak to either.** §28 is at printed 361–367 and the Part III §2/§3 cut is at printed 416, both far outside the apograph's *usque ad pag. 250*. **Those two boundaries remain exactly as well or badly supported as they were, and this pass adds nothing to them.** They stand on our own reasoning — which, in §3's case, was independently confirmed from the plate (the half-blank page at 424) and does not need the manuscript.

Everything below therefore concerns **Part I only**.

## 2. ⚠⚠ The three blank leaves after printed 184

This is the finding of the class-B pass.

**What our transcript has at the foot of printed 184:**

> καὶ ὁ θάνατος οὐκ ἔσται ἔτι, / οὔτε κραυγὴ, / οὔτε πόνος οὐκ ἔσται ἔτι, / **ὅτι τὰ πρῶτα ἀπῆλθον.**

Revelation xxi. 4 — *and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away.* **A terminal text if the book has one.** And the manuscript answers it with **six blank pages**, more air than it gives anything else.

**What follows, at printed 186:** `ΑΓΑΘΥΝΟΝ, ΚΥΡΙΕ` — the great personal intercession, Barking and Pembroke Hall and Southwell and Westminster, Andrewes' own churches under Greek names.

⚠ **Our own transcript already noticed that this is a different kind of thing.** The note at the head of printed 186 in `day7-transcript.md` reads *"the great personal intercession (cf. Day 3 pp. 98–101)"* — it cross-references it out of Day 7 to a different day, because it does not belong where it sits. **The manuscript agrees, and says so with three blank leaves; our file runs straight through it.**

Likewise the two blank pages after **190. 4**, which fall immediately after the close of a list (`προαιρέσεις, / ἐπιχειρήσεις, / ἔξοδον, καὶ εἴσοδον, / κάθεδραν, καὶ ἔγερσιν.`) — a clean end, and the MS marks it.

**What this does NOT mean.** It does not mean `day7-transcript.md` is wrong. The file records the 1853 plate faithfully and the plate does run continuously. **The manuscript and the printed book disagree about where Day 7 ends, and that disagreement is a fact about the edition, not an error in our files.** Whether to re-cut the file, or to record the division in the apparatus and leave the file alone, is **Wilson's call** — and it is the same question the Hebrew raises in `CLASS-A-ledger.md` §2: is this edition a faithful reprint of the 1853, or a critical text of Andrewes?

**A pragmatic note for whoever decides:** re-cutting a transcript re-cuts its English, and Part I's English is line-keyed page by page. The cost is real. The cheaper and probably better move is to **record the MS's divisions in the apparatus and in the print design** — Prototype B has spreads to spend, and six blank manuscript pages are exactly the sort of thing a mirror edition can honour typographically without touching a transcript.

## 3. ⚠ `p. 207` — the only odd page in class B

Every other citation in classes A and B is an **even** printed page, because Part I sets Greek on the verso (even) and Latin on the recto (odd), and the apograph is Greek only. **`p. 207. pagina non scripta sequitur` is the sole exception in the entire apparatus.**

Three possibilities, and this file does not choose between them:
1. a misprint for **206** or **208** — the likeliest, and the volume has proved itself capable of exactly this class of slip repeatedly;
2. the editor briefly citing by his own **Latin** page because that is where the leaf-break falls;
3. something about the manuscript's own foliation at that point.

**Left as a crux.** It costs nothing and resolves nothing to guess.

## 4. What class B corroborates rather than challenges

**The seven days.** Class B does **not** mark the day-divisions with spacing — and it should not, because the preface's principle **iv** says *`Dies sua quisque nota astronomica distinguitur`*: **each day is distinguished by its own astronomical sign**, a different device entirely. **Our day boundaries at printed 30 · 70 · 92 · 106 · 130 · 148 · 168 are corroborated by principle iv and are untouched by class B's silence about them.** This is worth stating plainly, because a naive reading of class B would take the absence of spacing at those pages as evidence against our cuts. It is not.

**The deprecation/evening cut at 218–220** is confirmed outright by two blank pages.

## 5. The ordinary spacing record — for the typesetter, not the editor

The bulk of class B (apparatus vii–ix) is a line-by-line register of ordinary spaces, larger spaces, and lines run together without space: e.g. *`p. 124. lin. 3, 4, 6, 7, 11, 12, 17, 18, 24, 25, sine intervallo`*; *`p. 172. post lin. 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, et post 14 et 25 majus`*; *`p. 36. post lin. 1, 5 majus, 18, 22, 25, 29`*. It distinguishes at least three weights — *spatium* · *majus spatium / majus intervallum* · *breve spatium* — and records where lines the plate separates are **joined** (*colligantur*, 192. 11–13 and 14–16) or where lines the plate joins are **separated**.

⚠ **This is the single most useful document in the apparatus for Prototype B**, and it is entirely unexploited. The mirror's whole difficulty is page-fit: every spread must fit or the verso/recto mirror silently flips. **Class B is a page-by-page account, from the author's own book, of where the vertical air is meant to go and how much of it** — which is precisely the variable a typesetter has to guess at otherwise.

**Recommendation, for a later print session and not for now:** when `transcript2tex.py` is finally generalised, feed class B in as a spacing table keyed to printed page and line. It will not solve page-fit, but it converts an arbitrary choice into a documented one, and where a spread will not fit, it says which spaces Andrewes cared about and which he did not.
