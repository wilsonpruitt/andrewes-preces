- ⚠⚠ **314: the list of forty-odd sufferings ends on the ONE item with NO proof-text** — *Ἄγνωστοι βάσανοι*, torments unknown, number 10, nothing against it. **He assembles the whole Passion out of Scripture and then closes by admitting Scripture does not hold it. The blank is the point; supply nothing.**
- **314–315: the wounds are made to FORGIVE, member for member** — wounded head forgives the senses of the head, hands dug through forgive unlawful touch, side forgives the heart's thoughts, feet forgive *the going of feet swift to evil*. Then at 315 it turns — *et ego, Domine, vulneratus sum animâ* — and Paul's four dimensions of the love of Christ measure **the injury** instead, closing ***et per Tua sana mea***, by Thy wounds heal mine. **This is why the anatomy must stay exact on both sides.**
- **315 hands the *Allegatio*'s method over:** seven grounds named, then rather than pleading them he asks **Christ to offer them to the Father for him.**
- ⚠ **Printed 312 was TRIMMED before commit** (552pt against 531) by tightening its two `R:` notes, no claim dropped.

### 316–317
- ⚠ **316 quotes the *Veni Creator Spiritus* ENTIRE** — first stanza, the Pentecost/ordination hymn — in a section that has proved every other article out of Scripture. **Our English renders it as verse-shaped prose and must NOT import an existing English translation of the hymn**: the *Preces* is quoting a Latin text, and a familiar rendering makes the reader hear a hymnbook where the page has a prayer.
- ⚠ **A THIRD marginal register runs 316–317** — single words hard left naming the *mode* of each coming: **Shadow · Dove · Breath · Fiery tongues.** Same class as *Shame*/*Pain* at 313 and the orders-and-gifts columns at 304. **The plate's, structural, and never part of the line they stand against.**
- **317: *invocatio*/*advocatio* only works because of the Greek beneath it** — Ἐπίκλησις and Παράκλησις are **one verb (καλέω) with different prefixes**, a calling-upon and a calling-alongside, so the Spirit invoked and the Spirit as Advocate are one act from two ends. English can't imitate it. **Same-line gloss → §41: render ONCE with the Greek visible.**
- **317: the catalogue borrows its shape from the verse it cites** — *gifts, ministrations, operations* is 1 Cor. xii. 4–6 entire (each assigned to a different Person), used as the frame for the gifts/works/fruits that follow. The three bare words are **a heading, not a summary.**

### 318–319
- ⚠⚠ **319 is the clearest case in Part II for the edition's whole method.** A ladder of Greek prefixes — περισσεία, πλεονασμός, then **ὑπερ**πλεονασμός, **ὑπερ**περισσεία, **ὑπερ**βαλλόντως — each compound taken from a place where Paul reached for a word that did not exist till he made it. **The rhetoric IS the repetition of ὑπερ-**, which English must render four different ways (super-, over-, exceeding, surpassing), **so the ladder flattens the moment the Greek is dropped.** Plate keeps Greek beside Latin; our English keeps both, glossing not replacing.
- ⚠ **Paul's four dimensions measure MERCY at 319 and measured the WOUND at 315.** *Μῆκος, Πλάτος, Βάθος* = Eph. iii. 18, filled from the Psalter (from everlasting **to** everlasting = length; up to heaven, down to hell = depth). **Fourth instance in this Part of a text used twice and never for the same thing. Do not conform or cross-refer.**
- **318:** a second Latin word for three of the seven works — **refinements, not synonyms** (*Invisere cor* names what is visited; ***Perductio*** says where the perfecting arrives). ⚠ **This column is Latin glossing LATIN**, unlike 316–317's *Shadow · Dove · Breath* which named a mode.
- ⚠ **318's *Triumph of Mercy* numbers continuously 1–13 ACROSS its braces** — a brace is a grouping inside one series, **not a series of its own; renumbering within a brace breaks the count.**

### 320 — the densest leaf in the book
- ⚠ **Twelve cases in a row, every one named by the thing that DISQUALIFIED him** — not Peter but *Peter that denied*; *Thomas the doubter*, *Paul the blasphemer*, *Magdalene the sinner*, the son *wicked*, *Jonah that fled*, the woman *taken*, the thief. Where no person is named, the case is: the sheep that strayed, the coin lost, the debtor of ten thousand talents, the man left half dead. **It could have been a roll of saints and is deliberately a roll of failures**, because the heading is *Mercy* and the argument is what mercy has in fact done. **Keep every epithet — dropping one turns an argument back into a commemoration.**
- **Mercy is given a place and a time, and the place is a piece of furniture.** *Propitiatorium* = the **mercy seat**, the ark's gold cover between the cherubim (*there I will meet with thee*); Hebrews' *throne of grace* is that same seat read as a throne — **one object under two names, OT and NT**. The time answers in the same shape: *the day of salvation*, which is **now**.

### 321–322
- ⚠⚠ **A SECOND independent instance of one verse numbered two ways across the Parts.** *I am a stranger with thee, and a sojourner* = `Psal. xxxix. **12**` at 322 and `Psal. xxxix. **14**` at printed 261. Twelve is the **AV** exactly; fourteen answers no system cleanly (BCP = 13), **so Part I's may be a slip of one rather than another Psalter — the PAIRING is the finding.** Same phenomenon as the pierced hands and feet (154 = `xxii. 17` BCP, 312 = `xxii. 16` AV, there unambiguous). **Two independent instances now. Never reconcile a figure to another.**
- ⚠ **321: a promise about God's WORD is turned on the man's own prayer.** *Non redeat ad me vacua* is Isa. lv. 11, of **the word going out from God's mouth**; Andrewes asks it of the prayer going up from his. **Traffic reversed, guarantee borrowed** — he does not argue for the transfer and the English does not soften it; the next clause (*but as Thou knowest, and canst, and wilt*) is what stands in place of an argument.
- **321: two chains of six answering verb for verb** (ascend·reach·enter·appear·find grace·draw near ‖ hear·incline·attend·understand·hearken·**remember to do**), all twelve proof-texted, ⚠ **and the second does not end at hearing** — *memento ut facias* is Daniel's, and the sequence is incomplete until the hearing becomes an act.
- **322's warding-off ends on a deliberately LOPSIDED antithesis** — *let the Lion be conquered by the weak Sheep, the violent spirit by the feeble flesh*: adversary named by strength, petitioner by weakness, **verb passive in both halves** (nothing is asked to fight). **Keep the diminutive and both adjectives — the disproportion IS the prayer.**

### 323 — and the dense run closes
- ⚠ **The confession is made ENTIRELY in other men's words.** Eleven numbered admissions, not one Andrewes' own sentence: *I have sinned, I have done unjustly* (Psalter), *I have dealt wickedly in the covenant* (Solomon, putting words in the mouth of a people not yet carried away), *I have grieved the Spirit* (Isaiah, of the wilderness generation), *I have gone after my own thoughts* (Jeremiah, quoting the people's defiance). **Each is a third-person or plural indictment turned into the FIRST PERSON SINGULAR — the accusation accepted rather than answered.** Same method as 313's Passion catalogue: *proving*, not describing.
- ⚠ **323 carries the reference the volume elsewhere gets wrong.** `Job xiv. 4` is correct here; at printed **406** Part III prints `Job. xiv. **14**` (*if a man die, shall he live again?*) for the same material. **Both stand as printed.** Fourth self-corroboration, with `Is. xxxiii. 15`↔§37, `Psal. cxxx. 30`↔297, and 25↔409.
