## Transcription notes

1. **Offset +43 across all six leaves**, eye-verified, no duplicates. ⚠ **From printed 433 the offset becomes +45** — the spread 431/432 is scanned twice (PDF 474/475, then 476/477) — so the two sections after this one need their leaves checked one by one. Recorded in STRUCTURE's anchor table.

2. **⚠ The tall braces (428–430) are the largest in the volume and the cue words have been moved.** *Quod* stands vertically centred beside a **thirteen-line** brace at 428, *compos;* beside the middle of a nested three-line brace inside it, and *vel* and *et* between facing brace-columns at 430. A text file cannot centre a word beside a thirteen-line brace, so **each cue is written on the first line of its group** (*Quod {*, *Pro {*) — the convention already in force for centred cues since Part II §1 — **except** the two facing-column groups at 430, which use the §32/§36 ` | ` exception with the cue in a middle column so that the row count matches the plate exactly. `transcript2tex.py` will need to restore the vertical centring; the line count, not the cue's position, is what this file guarantees.

3. **⚠ Two odd readings at printed 425, both kept as printed and neither explained:**
   - **`*Prov.* x. 25.`** for *Anima quæ benedixit, impinguabitur* — the verse is Prov. **xi**. 25. Joins the running list of wrong references.
   - **`Cantemus Domino Isaac et Israelitarum`** (Exod. xv. 2) — the song at the Red Sea is Moses' and Israel's, and **Isaac has no business in it**. It stands beside *Benedictus Deus Jethronis* (Exod. xviii. 10), which is at least a real blessing spoken by Jethro. Whether *Isaac* is a compositor's error, a scribe's, or a compression of something else **cannot be settled from these files**; transcribed as printed and left as a question. **On the pre-print verification list.**

4. **The reference column carries verse-only continuations.** At 426–427 a bare numeral in the margin (`14.` `15.` `10.` `3.` `4.` `5.`) continues the book and chapter of the reference above it, and at 425 the `4.` beside *totius consolationis* continues 2 Cor. i. 3 mid-sentence. Transcribed as bare numerals exactly as set; **a later pass must not expand them**, since the expansion would double the apparatus.

5. **No `[?]`, no Greek, no Hebrew.** Six clean leaves.
