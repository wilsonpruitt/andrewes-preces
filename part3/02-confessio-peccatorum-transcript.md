Source: 1853 Parker ed., printed pp. 406–416 (upper), images `raw/end/p-NNN.jpg`. Offset **+41 for printed 406–412 (PDF 447–453), then +43 from printed 413 (PDF 456)** — every leaf eye-verified. Signatures `AND. PRECES. — 3 G` at printed 409, `3 H` at 417. **LATIN throughout**, no Greek and no Hebrew.

⚠ **The spread 412/413 is scanned TWICE, and the first scan of 413 (PDF 454) is CUT OFF at the right margin** — page number gone, six lines truncated mid-word. **Printed 413 below is transcribed from the complete duplicate at PDF 456.** The truncation is a scan artifact, not a plate defect: the duplicate reads whole, and the OCR agrees with it.

**⚠ A LEFT-MARGIN REFERENCE COLUMN, new to the volume, begins at the last line of printed 407 and runs to the end of the section.** In Parts I and II every scripture reference is set inline, trailing its line. Here most references are set **in the left margin, on their own, level with the line they govern**, with the text in a hanging block to the right — and the two styles alternate on the same page. **Encoding: a reference at the START of a line was printed in the margin column; a reference trailing its line was printed inline.** That distinction is lossless, keys 1:1 with the English, and must survive into the typesetting — `transcript2tex.py` has never met this layout, and at the foot of printed 410 the column is **offset by one line from the text it governs**, which is transcribed exactly as set and must not be silently re-aligned.

Transcription discipline: verbatim incl. 1853 accentuation and punctuation; `[?]` = unreadable; indentation = 4 spaces per printed level; book abbreviations italicized exactly as printed; `[ ]` = the 1853 editor's supplied references; braces per CONVENTIONS §2–4; row-spanning brace columns as ` | ` per the §32/§36 exception. Running heads, catchwords and printer's signatures omitted.

## Transcription notes

1. **Offset.** +41 for printed 406–412 (PDF 447–453); **+43 from printed 413** (PDF 456), after the twice-scanned 412/413 spread. Every leaf eye-verified against its own running head; `3 G` at 409 and `3 H` at 417 confirm the count independently.

2. **⚠ Printed 413's first scan (PDF 454) is truncated at the right margin** — the page number and roughly six line-endings are lost (*quc / creasti / servare n( / Inimicus ravit / et p( / T*). The duplicate at **PDF 456 is complete**, and the OCR of the complete leaf agrees with it. **Not a watch item** — unlike printed 239 (watch item 1), the defect here is repaired inside the same scan set. Recorded so that a later pass does not "discover" the truncation and transcribe `[?]`.

3. **The reference column** — see the header. It begins at the last line of printed 407 (`*Is.* lii. 8.   Lata,`) and runs to the end. Two places need care in typesetting: the `Gravia instar { oneris. / plumbi. }` line at 408, where a margin reference and an inline brace share a line; and **the foot of printed 410**, where the four margin references sit **one line off** from the text they govern (`*Psal.* cxix. 94.` stands beside *quare serva me*, whose sentence begins on the line above; `*Sap.* xi. 25.` beside *despexeris*, likewise). **Transcribed exactly as set — do not re-align.**

4. **⚠ SIX WRONG REFERENCES, all kept as printed** — this is much the densest concentration in the volume, and **two of them are proved wrong by the 1853 itself**:
   - **`Job. xiv. 14.` (406)** for *conceptus sum in immundo semine*. The verse is **Job xiv. 4** — and **§17 prints exactly the same matter with the correct `Job xiv. 4.`** (printed 323).
   - **`Is. xxxiii. 15.` (409)** for *Et quid dicam jam? … cum ipse hæc fecerim?* The verse is **Isaiah xxxviii. 15** — and **§37 prints the same sentence with the correct `[Is. xxxviii. 15.]`** (printed 372). Note also that Part III reads *cum ipse **hæc** fecerim* once where §37 has *fecerim, fecerim, fecerim* three times; that is a recension difference, not an error, and neither is conformed to the other.
   - **`[Marc. iv. 28.]` (410)** for *ad Te non pertinet, si perimus?* — Mark iv. **38**.
   - **`Jo. ix. 30.` (411)** for *peccatores non audit Deus* — John ix. **31**.
   - **`lxviii. 39.` (411)** for *quod caro, et quod ventus qui transit, nec revertitur* — Psalm **lxxviii**. 39, and the verse §1 of Part II already uses.
   - **`1 Cor. xi. 30.` (413)** for *Sed dijudico ipse me* — 1 Cor. xi. **31**.
   All six verified against the OCR, so none is a misreading of the plate. They join the running list (`[Jer. l. 24.]` §23, `[Eph. i. 8.]` §27, `[Dan. vi. 4.]` §28, `[Psal. cxix. 114.]` §34, `ante Intercessionum` §40, `Epistola xxvii. lib. x.` §42, `peccata meam` Part III §1).

6. **The double brace at printed 412.** *Pulvis / Ventus / Caro* are gathered by a right-facing brace and *fragilis / levis / dissolutus* by a left-facing one — two brace columns facing each other, the §32/§36 ` | ` exception. Encoded row-wise. The natural pairing runs **across** (dust is frail, wind is light, flesh dissolves), but the double brace deliberately makes every member available to every other; the English keeps the rows and does not close the figure.

7. **No `[?]`, no Greek, no Hebrew.** Eleven leaves, all clean apart from the truncated duplicate noted above.
