## Translator's flags

1. **Parity 2/2, line-exact** — 27 lines against 27 on printed 431, 31 against 31 on printed 432. Turned lines are reproduced where the English can carry them (`turn-/est`, `eye-/lids`, `iniqui-/ties`, `medi-/tation`), per the Part I and §4 practice.

2. **The Psalter crib yields to the Latin in four places, and each is the Vulgate standing where Coverdale stands elsewhere** (CONVENTIONS §9's now-general principle: the rule governs lines Andrewes took from the LXX/Vulgate and yields wherever the plate's Latin has gone another way):
   - *auroram* → **"the dawn"**, not the Prayer Book's "thou hast prepared the light."
   - *a negotio perambulante in tenebris* → **"the thing that walketh about in darkness"**, not "the pestilence."
   - *a dæmone meridiano* → **"the noonday devil"**, not "the sickness that destroyeth in the noon-day." Kept as the plate's figure, not softened.
   - *a manu venantium* → **"from the hand of the hunters"**, not "from the snare of the hunter" — the plate reads *manu* where the Vulgate reads *laqueo*.

3. **`in opera ipsorum` is translated "upon their own works"** and the emphasis is deliberate. The Vulgate has *opera Tua*; the plate turns the verse so that God looks on His servants' works rather than His own. The change is the plate's and it is not repaired, in either layer.

4. **`ostium circumstantiæ labiis meis` is rendered "a door round about my lips"**, keeping the Vulgate's odd genitive rather than the Prayer Book's "keep the door of my lips." *Circumstantia* is the Vulgate's calque and Andrewes prays it as it stands.

5. **`[Te Deum]` is kept as a source-tag, not converted to a scripture reference**, and the English of its line follows the Prayer Book's own Te Deum — "Vouchsafe, O Lord, to keep us this day without sin" — which is what Andrewes' *nos hodie* is already reaching for.

6. **The references are reproduced as printed and are wrong in four places** (Ps. 74:6 for 74:16 · Ps. 119:81 for 119:91 · Ps. 134:4 for 132:4 · the Ps. 143 run numbering one high from its second member). Nothing is corrected here; the corrections are argued in the transcript's note 3 and belong to the apparatus, not to the text.
