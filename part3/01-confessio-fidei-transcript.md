## Transcription notes

1. **Offset +41, clean, eight leaves, no duplicates.** Printed 400–405 all read off their own plates (PDF 441–446). PDF 439 and 440 carry no page number (an epigraph leaf and a section-opener leaf), which is the 1853's normal practice; they are printed 398 and 399 by the bracketing.

2. **⚠ `Ponam peccata meam coram me` (398) — a wrong sort, kept as printed.** *meam* for *mea*; verified at 600 dpi (`raw/end/crop-ponam-439-439.jpg`), the *m* is fully formed and not a broken *a*. The same sentence stands correctly as *peccata **mea** coram me* at §38 (printed 380). Kept, flagged, not mended — joins `[Jer. l. 24.]` §23, `[Eph. i. 8.]` §27, `[Dan. vi. 4.]` §28, `[Psal. cxix. 114.]` §34, `ante Intercessionum` §40, `Epistola xxvii. lib. x.` §42.

3. **`Eccl.` for Ecclesiasticus (398).** The reference is Ecclus. xxxv. 17 (Vulg. xxxv. 21). Part II abbreviates the book *Ecclus.* (§40, printed 383) and *Eccl.* is elsewhere Ecclesiastes; the plate's `Eccl.` is kept.

4. **The top-level article numbers begin at 3.** *In Deum* (399) and *In Jesum Christum* (399–401) carry no article number; the numerals `3.` (In Spiritum Sanctum) and `4.` (Sanctam Catholicam Ecclesiam) at printed 401 imply them retrospectively. Nothing supplied. Note the numeral series `1. 2. 3.` under *In Deum* and `1.`–`12.` under *In Jesum* are a **different, inner** series — the plate re-uses the figures at two depths, and the indentation is the only thing distinguishing them. Encoded by indent exactly as set.

5. **Turned-line hyphens (403–404):** *infer-/num*, *novita-/tem*, *quæ-/rentes*, *dijudi-/cantes*. These are the compositor's justification, unlike §43's poetic word-splits, and `transcript2tex.py` may re-flow them.

6. **Braces.** Three only, all inline: `quia { Creator, / Redemptor, }` (400), `in { conceptione et / nativitate; }` (402), and the trailing-tail brace `Ad spem { Resurrectionis, / Translationis, } ad vitam æternam.` (405) — the last is set as a true two-row brace with *ad vitam æternam* level with the brace, i.e. governing both members. No row-spanning ` | ` tables in this section.

7. **No Greek and no Hebrew in this section** — the first stretch of the volume since printed 331–333 (§21) that is Latin without a single Greek word.

8. **No `[?]`.** All eight leaves are clean; the Latin `_djvu.txt` finding aid agrees with the vision transcription throughout, including at *meam* (398) and *subministratorem* (405).
